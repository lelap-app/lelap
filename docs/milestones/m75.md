# M75 Podcasts 3

Podcast work left out of M23, M47 and M53.

- **Soundbites.** `podcast:soundbite` is read per episode (`soundbitesOf` in
  `packages/core/src/podcasts/podcasting.ts`). The podcast page counts them in the episode's line,
  and holding the episode lists each one; a tap plays the episode from its start
  (`playSoundbite` in `apps/app/src/podcasts.ts`).
- **Place.** `podcast:location` of the show and of each episode is stored; the podcast page shows
  the show's under its author.
- **Value for value.** `podcast:value` blocks and their recipients are stored with the show and
  the episode. Nothing is shown and no money is sent.
- **Alternate enclosures.** An item without an audio `<enclosure>` plays an audio
  `podcast:alternateEnclosure` at its first web address, the one marked `default` first
  (`alternateAudioOf` in `rss.ts`). A video show that lists its audio this way now plays audio.
- **Atom text.** `<content>`, `<summary>` and `<subtitle>` with `type="xhtml"` are kept raw by the
  XML parser and read as text, nested elements and all (`atomTextOf` in `xml.ts`).
- **Logging from the background refresh.** The background task reads the diagnostics switch
  (`loadDiagnosticsSwitch` in `apps/app/src/diagnostics.ts`). `refreshFeeds` reports which feeds
  failed, and `feedRefreshEvents` in `packages/core/src/diagnostics/build.ts` turns that into a
  `podcast` event: a summary with counts for every background run, plus up to five failed feeds
  by row ID, reason and HTTP status. A refresh started with the app open is logged only when a
  feed failed, so the log doesn't say when someone opens the app. A background run that fails
  logs an `app` error.

Tests: `packages/core/src/podcasts/tags.test.ts` with the made-up Garden Hour feeds in
`packages/core/test/fixtures/garden-hour.xml` and `garden-hour.atom`, and the podcast events in
`packages/core/src/diagnostics/diagnostics.test.ts`.
