# M88 Podcasts 4

Left out of M75, plus what M85 left for here.

- **Transcripts following along** were already on `main` (M23): the player's Transcript button
  opens `app/transcript.tsx`, which lights the line playing, follows it and seeks on a tap.
- **Chapter images.** A JSON chapters file's `img` shows in the player in place of the cover while
  its stretch plays, `toc: false` entries included, since they exist to change the picture
  (`parseChapterImages` and `chapterImageAt` in `packages/core/src/podcasts/podcasting.ts`;
  `useChapterImage` in `apps/app/src/podcasting.ts`). The chapters file is fetched once per app
  start for both its chapters and its pictures; the web app reads it through the proxy as before.
  A picture on plain http goes through the proxy's `/cover` on the web, like any cover.
- **Clips that stop at their end.** `playClip` in `apps/app/src/clips.ts` plays a stretch and
  pauses at its end; `ClipStop` (mounted in `app/_layout.tsx`) watches the player's place and
  sets a timer for the end (`clipStep` in `packages/core/src/clip.ts`). Seeking out of the stretch
  or playing another book ends the clip, and playback goes on as usual. Soundbites play this way
  from the podcast page and from the player, and so does a shared clip from `/place`.
- **Soundbites in the player.** An episode with soundbites gets a Soundbites button in the player's
  row, which lists them with their times (`SoundbitesSheet`).
- **Shared places in episodes.** An episode's place, bookmark or last 30 seconds can be shared
  like a book's. The link names the episode by its show's feed and its guid (`f` and `g` in the
  query) with the episode and show titles. An episode is not shared when its feed has a login, a
  query or a path segment that looks like a token (a UUID, or 20 or more letters and digits), where
  private feeds keep their key, or when it has no guid of its own, since its guid is then the
  audio's address (`isShareableFeed` and `placeOfEpisode` in `packages/core/src/placeLink.ts`).
  Opening it finds the episode on the device (`findPlaceEpisode`). A show this profile doesn't
  subscribe to is subscribed from the feed with Subscribe and play; a subscribed show whose stored
  feed lacks the episode (it may be new) is read again with Refresh and play
  (`playSharedEpisode` in `apps/app/src/podcasts.ts`). A kids profile only finds an episode on its
  shelf. See “Shared places” in the [brief](../brief.md).
- **Known limits.** On Android, JavaScript timers wait while the app is in the background, so with
  the screen off a clip stops on the player's next once-a-second update, up to about a second
  late. On the web, Subscribe and play may wait long enough for a browser to refuse to start
  playing by itself; the listener then presses play.

Left out, in [M103](m103.md): choosing among alternate enclosures, checking `podcast:integrity`,
and chapter images on the lock screen.

New requests: none for the transcript (unchanged). The player now reads an episode's chapters file
for its pictures even when the audio has its own chapters, and the pictures load from the
addresses the feed's chapters file gives. Opening a shared episode link fetches the show's feed
only after Subscribe and play or Refresh and play.

Tests: `packages/core/src/clip.test.ts`, the chapter images in
`packages/core/src/podcasts/podcasting.test.ts` and the episode places in
`packages/core/src/placeLink.test.ts`, with the made-up Garden Hour feed.
