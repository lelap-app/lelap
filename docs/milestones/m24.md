# M24 Stats and goals

While something plays, the app adds the position's advance in real time (never more than the time
that passed, so seeks don't count) to a per-day log in `listening_log`
(`packages/core/src/stats.ts`). The Stats screen (from Home) shows today, the last 7 days as bars,
this year, the streak and the most listened books, plus books finished this year against an
optional goal. The log and the goal stay on the device and are never synced. Time the playback
service hears with the app swiped away is counted since [M51](m51.md).

Done when a week of normal listening on the phone shows the same hours the listener expects.
