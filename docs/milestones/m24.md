# M24 Stats and goals

**Status:** in progress (from [`next.md`](../next.md#proposed-milestones)). While something plays,
the app adds the position's advance in real time (never more than the time that passed, so seeks
don't count) to a per-day log in `listening_log`. The Stats screen (from Home) shows today, the
last 7 days as bars, this year, the streak and the most listened books, plus books finished this
year against an optional goal. Everything is computed and kept on the device: the log and the goal
are never synced. Phone checks are in [`m24-follow-ups.md`](../m24-follow-ups.md).

**Done when** a week of normal listening on the phone shows the same hours the listener expects.
