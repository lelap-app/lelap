# M113 Big screens 2

[M44](m44.md) gave tablets and laptops a rail and two panes. This milestone is the next step for
foldables, tablets and the desktop web app.

- **Foldables:** follow the fold (Jetpack WindowManager's posture through an Expo module, or the
  window size alone if that is enough): the player on one half and the Library on the other when
  half open; no control under the hinge.
- **Wide desktop windows:** a third column on very wide windows (the Library, a book's page and the
  player side by side) where the width allows it.
- **Resizable panes:** drag the divider between panes, remembered per device.
- **Drag to a list:** on the web and with a mouse, drag a book or episode onto a list in the rail.
- **Check every screen** at the widths `packages/core/src/screen.ts` decides, so no screen is left
  stretched across a 1920 px window.
