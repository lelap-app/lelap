# M71 App polish

UI findings left from earlier reviews, fixed where they were still real:

- **Places saved with the app closed show at once.** The player folds the service's journal in
  before the screens draw, waiting no longer than 1.5 s (`PlayerProvider` in
  `apps/app/src/player.tsx`). (M2)
- **No unhandled rejections** at start-up, from Settings → Rescan or from the folder picker in
  Settings and the Library: they show a message or go to the diagnostics log (`logCaught` in
  `apps/app/src/diagnostics.ts`). (M2)
- **The skip buttons show the skip interval** (10–60 s) in the Player and car mode, not always
  30 (`skipBack` and `skipForward` in `Icon.tsx` take the number). (M2)
- **A regrouped book keeps playing in the Player.** After a scan regroups the loaded book's files,
  the player loads the book that now holds the file, at the same place (`followRegrouped`, with
  `regroupedBook` in `packages/core/src/library/bookFiles.ts`). (M2)
- **The Library's spinner shows only when something happens:** for a pull, the rescan button or
  a scan that reads files; the scan at each start that finds nothing new stays out of sight. (M2)
- **Books appear root by root.** Each folder or server's books show as soon as its scan is in the
  database, not when every root is done (`onRootScanned` in `apps/app/src/scan.ts`). Within one
  root, books still wait for its tags. (M2)
- **The Continue card names the chapter** for books with more than one (`chapterTitleAt`). (M2)
- **A rescan button in the Library's header**, whenever there is a folder or a server. (M2)
- **Discover keeps its place** when the player pane opens or the window resizes: it lays out its
  rows itself instead of with `numColumns`, which needed a new list each time. (M44)
- **Tiles and rows don't redraw for the library with badges off.** Covers read their badge source
  from its own context (`BadgeSourceProvider` in `CoverBadges.tsx`), which stays the same while
  badges are off. (M49)
- **The item page's cover shows the same ✓ or percentage as its tile** for a book matched by title
  and author. (M49)
- **An add-on whose settings can't be opened here says so** on its row in Add-ons: enter the
  settings again; events wait for up to 30 days. (M61)
- **Pressed chips show their pressed colour again:** `Pill` applies its `active:` classes itself.
  (M42)
- **“Allow”, “Continue” and “Try again” are semibold again** (`TextButton`'s `strong`). (M42)
- **The target checker counts real heights:** `py-3` around one line of text is about 44 points
  and needs `min-h-12`; with two lines it is tall enough. The two rows it found, in the action
  sheets, got `min-h-12`. (M42)
- **The diagnostics text wraps at large font sizes**, a size larger, instead of scrolling
  sideways. (M47)
- **Media Session shows covers kept in OPFS** on the web, as a blob URL once it is ready. (M67)

Already fine on `main`: `let listed` in `src/scan.ts` has its type (M2).

Moved to [M80](m80.md): the zoomed scrubber (M39), suggestion covers deeper in a page (M46), a
“new” badge for podcasts (M49), the sign-in and Plex library items (M59), a refused login's reason
in the player (M62), the web save's whole-database hash and the old “001 …” chapters (M67), reading
only changed books (M43), saving a matched cover (M56) and Smart AudioBook Player bookmarks (M34).
