# M71 App polish

UI findings that were left for later. None needs a new screen.

- The journal is folded in after the first render, not before. (M2)
- Unhandled rejections in start-up, Settings → Rescan and the folder picker. (M2)
- The skip buttons always say 30 although the interval can be 10–60 s. (M2)
- After a rescan regroups the loaded book, the Player says “Nothing is playing” until it reloads. (M2)
- `let listed` in `src/scan.ts` is an implicit `any`. (M2)
- The pull-to-refresh spinner shows even when a scan reads nothing. (M2)
- Books appear only when a scan ends. (M2)
- The Continue card has no chapter. (M2)
- No rescan button in the Library’s header. (M2)
- Zoom: a long press on the time opens a magnified bar around the playhead (±5 minutes). (M39)
- Discover rebuilds its grid and scrolls to the top when the pane appears or the column count changes. (M44)
- Suggestions deeper than the first ten of a page show the title’s letter, not a cover; covers could come back once Home has loaded them. (M46)
- Every tile and row listens to the library signal, so all redraw on a library change, even with badges off; pass the badge source down from the list. (M49)
- A tile can show ✓ or a percentage for a folder book matched by title and author, while the item page’s cover shows none. (M49)
- The Podcasts tab and podcast page draw no badges; unplayed counts could become a “new” badge. (M49)
- A sign-in result that arrives while the listener edits the server’s addresses replaces them. (M59)
- Plex’s “Libraries” setting takes names; a list to pick from needs the server reachable before sign-in finishes. (M59)
- When an add-on’s settings can’t be opened (after a restore on another phone), events wait (503) for up to 30 days, and the add-on’s row doesn’t say so. (M61)
- A book that can’t play because of a refused login or a changed key shows the player’s usual error; the reason shows in Settings only after the next scan. (M62)
- `Pill` puts its classes on an inner view, so the pressed colour of tag and add-on chips no longer shows. (M42)
- `TextButton` has no weight option, so “Allow”, “Continue” and “Try again” lost their semibold. (M42)
- The target checker counts `py-3` with one line of text as tall enough; it is about 44 points. (M42)
- The diagnostics text is small and fixed in a sideways scroller, hard to read at large font sizes; a full-screen view could follow. (M47)

Each item above is fixed; the target checker counts real heights.
