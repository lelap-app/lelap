# M119 Lists 3

What [M112](m112.md) left out when lists became something to play through. How a list plays away
from the phone is in the [brief](../brief.md#decisions) (“Playing a list”).

- **Lists in Android Auto:** a Lists shelf in the car's browse tree, up to 10 lists. Each opens on
  Play list, which starts where the list stands, then a window of its items (a few before that
  place, 12 in all) to start from. The service follows the list by itself, Up Next first as in the
  app, and leaves a note the app takes when it wakes (`takeListStarted`), so the app follows the
  same list, in the same order for a smart list. The app sends the lists with the car's shelves
  (`carLists` in `packages/core/src/car.ts`); the tree is in `CarLibrary.kt`.
- **Previous in list off the phone:** the notification's overflow, which the lock screen, watches
  and Android Auto show, has Previous in list beside Next in list while the list's own item plays.
  The app sends the list with the queue: its name, the item playing and up to 5 items before it
  (`QueueList`); the service keeps that list as it moves along, so going back works with the app
  closed. The item left comes next again, at its place (`ListPlay.kt`).
- **The list on the widget:** the tall widget's last line says “Next in list: …”, or which list
  plays at its last item. The small widget has no room for it.
- **The web's media keys stay the skip by time:** ⏮/⏭ in the browser's controls and on
  keyboards and headsets skip by time also while a list plays, as on Android, so they never lose
  the time skip; the list is stepped in the app's list banner. This keeps M112's decision (see the
  brief).
- **Lighter reads while a list plays:** a hand-made list and a playing smart list read only their
  own books (`listBooksById`), not the whole library, for the queue, the player's list line and
  the list screen. A smart list that is not playing still reads the library for its rule.

      with the item it starts at, then its items.
- [ ] (you) [car] With the app swiped away, Play list in the car starts where the list stands;
      when the item ends the next one starts by itself, and opening the app afterwards shows the
      list as playing, with Playing and Next marked on its screen.
- [ ] (you) [car] An item picked in a list in the car plays from its place; a book in Up Next
      plays before the list goes on.
- [ ] (you) [car] A smart list started in the car (“Not started”) plays in the order the car
      showed, and the app follows that order when opened.
- [ ] (you) [car] While a list item plays in the car, the overflow has Previous in list and Next
      in list; Previous goes to the item before, and Next then comes back to the one left, at its
      place.
- [ ] (adb) While a list plays (its second item or later), the notification's overflow shows
      Previous in list; with the app swiped away it plays the item before, and pressing it again
      goes further back.
- [ ] (adb) While an Up Next book plays between two list items, the notification has Next in list
      but no Previous in list.
- [ ] (adb) The 4x2 widget shows “Next in list: <title>” while a list plays, and “From the list
      “<name>”” at its last item; the 4x1 widget is unchanged.
- [ ] (adb) After stopping the list with the player's ✕, the widget's list line and Previous in
      list leave.
- [ ] (you) Web, Chrome: while a list plays, ⏮/⏭ in the browser's media controls and the
      keyboard's media keys still skip by the interval; Previous and Next in the player's list
      banner step through the list.
- [ ] (adb) A large library (over 1,000 books) with a hand-made list playing: the player and the
      list screen stay quick when an item changes.
