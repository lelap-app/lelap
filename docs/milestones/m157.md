# M157 Folders as podcasts

A local folder of podcast episodes (the maintainer's Bandeland, 62 files) is one book today, with
each file as a chapter on one timeline. “Heard” then only means “before the place”, so an episode
heard out of order can't be marked, and the chapter list shows no difference between heard and not
heard. The decision is in the brief under
[Folders as podcasts](../brief.md#decisions); the screens were drawn on a design canvas first.

## Plan

- **Per-file state** (`packages/core`): each profile's library entry for a book gains whether it
  shows as a podcast and, per file, when it was played and where it was left. Synced with the
  entry, so older devices keep what they have.
- **Switching keeps progress.** Book → podcast: files before the place are played, the file being
  heard keeps its place. Podcast → book: the marks stay for a later switch back, and the book
  continues from the episode being heard, else the first unplayed one; when played episodes come
  after that place, the switch says so before it happens. Nothing is reset either way.
- **Playing** an episode stores where the last one was left and starts the chosen one at its own
  place. Reaching the end of a file marks it played; with the app open, the next unplayed episode
  plays instead of one already heard. The book counts as finished once every episode is played.
- **The show page** (`apps/app`): cover and title, “31 of 62 played” with a bar of one cell per
  episode, a Continue card, All, Unplayed and Played filters, oldest or newest first, seasons from
  the `S01E01` code in the file names, and per episode a circle that marks or unmarks it played.
  A long press plays, queues, marks the season or everything before as played.
- **Long press in the Library** on a book of several files: “Show as podcast” or “Show as book”.
  “This is a podcast…”, which links the folder to a feed, stays.

Done when Bandeland opens as a podcast with its played episodes marked, an episode heard out of
order can be marked by itself, and switching to book and back loses nothing.
