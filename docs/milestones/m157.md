# M157 Folders as podcasts

A book of several files, such as a local folder of podcast episodes, can be shown as a podcast by
a long press in the Library (“Show as podcast”, back with “Show as book”), per profile. The book
stays one book with one place, so the player, bookmarks and sync work as before; each file also
keeps its own played mark and the place it was left at. The decision is in the brief under
[Folders as podcasts](../brief.md#decisions).

## What shipped

- **Per-file state** (`packages/core/src/library/folderPodcast.ts`): `library_entries` gains
  `as_podcast` and `parts` (migration `0034`). `showAsPodcast` marks the files before the place
  played the first time; `showAsBook` continues from the episode being heard, else the first one
  not played, and `bookSwitch` counts the played episodes after that place for the warning. While
  a book that was once a podcast is shown as a book, files the place moves past are marked played
  (`noteFileChange`, called from `saveProgress`), so switching back loses nothing.
- **Playing**: `startEpisode` keeps where the last episode was left and starts the chosen one at
  its own place; a played one replays from the start, unmarked. A file left within 30 s of its end
  for the next one is played; from the service's journal, the files passed on the way count too.
  `skipPlayedEpisode` jumps over a played episode the player went on into, with the app open.
  `endOfPodcastBook` marks the last file at the book's end and moves the place to the first
  episode not played; the book is finished only once every episode is. Mark as played and
  unplayed on the whole book mark every episode (`setFinished`).
- **Sync**: the `entry` op carries `asPodcast` and `parts` by location and index; marks merge file
  by file, the newer change winning, also from an older entry. Older devices leave them out and
  keep what they have.
- **The show page** (`apps/app/src/components/FolderPodcastPage.tsx`, route `/folder`): played
  count and time left, a bar of one cell per episode by season, a Continue or Next unplayed card,
  All, Unplayed and Played filters, oldest or newest first, seasons from the `S01E01` codes in the
  file names (folded, the one being heard open), and per episode a mark that a tap sets or clears:
  a filled circle with a check, a ring with an arc as far as it was heard, or an empty ring. A
  long press plays, marks, marks the season or everything before as played. A tap in the Library
  opens this page instead of playing. `playFolderEpisode` in `apps/app/src/player.tsx` plays one.

Left for [M158](m158.md): skipping played episodes with the app closed, a single episode in Up
Next, swipe to mark, and the Library row's progress for a book shown as a podcast.
