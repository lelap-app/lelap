# M29 Sleep and links

The sleep timer can stop at the end of the chapter playing (books; the file's end without chapters)
or of the episode (podcasts, and Up Next does not start), with the same 60-second fade, exactly on
the boundary. It follows the chapter that is playing: playback never crosses the boundary, so it
only moves when the listener seeks or skips into another chapter. Pausing freezes it, and it shows
listening time at the current speed. Rules in `packages/core/src/sleep.ts` and `SleepBoundary.kt`
in `modules/audio`.

On Android a shake (three firm jolts within 0.8 s) in a timer's last five minutes adds ten minutes;
the accelerometer is on only then and needs no permission (`ShakeDetector.kt`). The web has no
shake, since browsers ask before sharing motion.

Expiring links: when an audio host answers 403 or 410, playback and downloads ask the stream's own
URL (an add-on's `/resolve/…` redirect) again, once, and continue at the same place
(`ExpiringLinks.kt`). The web player can't see HTTP statuses, so it asks again once after a network
or source error.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) **End of chapter, mid-file.** A single-file book with chapters: start “End of chapter” two
      minutes before a chapter mark. The moon shows `ch.` and the time left; the volume fades over
      the last minute; playback stops on the mark, and Play resumes a few seconds before it.
- [ ] (adb) **Seek moves it.** With a chapter timer running, pick a later chapter in the chapter list:
      the time left grows to that chapter's end. Skip back 30 s across a mark: it shrinks to the
      earlier chapter's end.
- [ ] (adb) **Speed.** At 2× the time left halves within a few seconds of changing speed.
- [ ] (adb) **App closed.** Swipe the app away with a chapter timer running: it still stops on the mark.
- [ ] (adb) **Battery.** With a 45-minute timer, the accelerometer is not listed as in use until the
      last five minutes (`adb shell dumpsys sensorservice`, active connections).
- [ ] (adb) **End of episode.** A podcast episode with another in Up Next: “End of episode” stops at
      the end, the episode is marked played, and the next one does not start.
- [ ] (adb) **Expired link, playback.** An add-on on the laptop (through `adb reverse`) whose
      `/resolve/…` URL redirects to a link that answers 403 after a minute: playback continues past
      the minute with at most a short buffering pause, and the position does not jump. A link that
      keeps answering 403 shows the error after one retry.
- [ ] (adb) **Expired link, download.** The same add-on with a download: the part file continues after
      the 403 instead of failing the download.

- [ ] (you) **Shake.** With a 5-minute timer, shake the phone (screen off, phone on a bed): ten minutes
      are added and the volume comes back. Turning over in bed must not trigger it; a shake with 20
      minutes left does nothing.
- [ ] (you) **Web.** In Chrome and Firefox: end of chapter mid-file, seek moving the timer and end of
      episode (the three timer checks above), and the expired link check for playback.
