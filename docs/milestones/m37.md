# M37 Kids profile

**Status:** in progress (built on M9's kids profiles).

M9 gave kids profiles their limits on add-ons: no installs or removals, and only the catalogs a
grown-up allows. M37 gives them their own library:

- **A shelf per kids profile.** Grown-ups put books on it from a book's menu (“Put on a kid’s
  shelf…”) and see and edit it in that profile's settings. It is a hidden hand-made list owned by
  the kids profile (`packages/core/src/kids.ts`), so it syncs like any list and needs no table.
- **What a kid sees.** Local files (on the phone or a server) only through the shelf; streamed
  books from the allowed catalogs once the kid starts them. Home's Continue listening and the
  Library follow the same rule (`listBooks`, `continueListening`).
- **What a kid can't change.** Add-ons (M9), servers, sync pairing, export and import, folders,
  podcast feed addresses and OPML. Kids subscribe to podcasts from the catalogs they are allowed.

A PIN on the grown-up profiles keeps a kid from switching to them (M9). Phone checks are in
[`m37-follow-ups.md`](../m37-follow-ups.md).

**Done when** a kid on a shared phone finds only their shelf and what they started, and can't
reach a grown-up's books, servers or add-ons without the grown-up's PIN.
