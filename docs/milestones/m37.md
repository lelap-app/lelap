# M37 Kids profile

M9 gave kids profiles their limits on add-ons (no installs or removals, only the catalogs a
grown-up allows); M37 gives them their own library.

- **A shelf per kids profile:** grown-ups put books on it from a book's menu (“Put on a kid’s
  shelf…”) and edit it in that profile's settings. It is a hidden hand-made list owned by the kids
  profile (`packages/core/src/kids.ts`), so it syncs like any list and needs no table.
- **What a kid sees:** local files (on the phone or a server) only through the shelf; streamed
  books from the allowed catalogs once the kid starts them. Home and the Library follow the same
  rule (`listBooks`, `continueListening`).
- **What a kid can't change:** add-ons, servers, sync pairing, export and import, folders, podcast
  feed addresses and OPML. A PIN on the grown-up profiles keeps a kid from switching to them.
