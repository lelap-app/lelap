# M26 Casting

`modules/cast` speaks open protocols only, so one build also suits F-Droid (see
[Decisions](../brief.md#decisions)):

- **Discovery** (`Discovery.kt`): SSDP for UPnP renderers and mDNS for `_googlecast._tcp` and
  `_fcast._tcp`, only while the Cast sheet is open.
- **UPnP** (`Upnp.kt`) for Sonos and DLNA renderers; **Google Cast** (`GoogleCast.kt`,
  `CastMessage.kt`), our own Cast v2 client for the Default Media Receiver, which doesn't check
  the device's TLS certificate (only control messages and LAN addresses cross it); **FCast**
  (`FCast.kt`).
- **Files on the phone** (`MediaServer.kt`): a server on the Wi-Fi address with Range support and
  one random token per file, only while casting. It also passes through streams that need a login
  (WebDAV), since receivers can't send headers.

Receivers fetch audio from its source. The Cast tool in the player picks the receiver; handing
back resumes on the phone at the receiver's place. [M50](m50.md) moved the session into the
playback service, with the lock screen, sleep timer, Up Next and UPnP speed.

Done when a book plays on a Sonos speaker, a Nest Mini and a DLNA TV, from a stream and from a file
on the phone, and handing back resumes on the phone at the same place.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) Sonos (One or Era): appears by room name; a LibriVox book streams and shows title and cover
      in the Sonos app; pause, seek, ±30 s, next chapter across a file boundary, volume.
- [ ] (you) Nest Mini: appears by its name; LAUNCH and LOAD work; speed 1.5× is applied; volume works;
      stopping from the Google Home app, or another phone taking the speaker, hands back.
- [ ] (you) A DLNA TV (Samsung, LG or Sony) plays an mp3 stream and an m4b from the phone; seek works once
      playback has started.
- [ ] (you) Local file over LAN: a downloaded book and a book from the library folder (`content://`) play
      from the phone's server; seeking far ahead works (Range); the server is gone after “Play on this
      phone” (the address then gives no answer).
- [ ] (you) A WebDAV book casts through the phone's pass-through.
- [ ] (you) The FCast desktop receiver plays, pauses and seeks.
- [ ] (you) Cast for a few minutes, swipe the app away, open it again: the place is within 10 s of where
      the receiver was. Hand back: the phone resumes at the receiver's place (smart rewind applies
      after a long cast, as after any pause).
- [ ] (you) Screen off for 30 minutes while casting a multi-part book: parts still advance.
- [ ] (you) On Wi-Fi with client isolation (guest networks): no receivers found, no crash.
- [ ] (you) A Google Cast speaker group appears as its own receiver and plays like a single speaker.
