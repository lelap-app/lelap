# M86 Sleep 2

Android only, like the sleep timer's shake and the bedtime timer before it. Decisions in the
brief: [Bedtime routine and After the sleep timer](../brief.md#decisions).

- **Shake for more time** is a setting (Settings → Playback, on by default, per profile). The
  accelerometer runs only while a timer counts down in its last five minutes, batched by the
  sensor hub so the app wakes a few times a second; `ShakeDetector` reads the samples' own
  timestamps (`PlaybackService`, `ShakeDetector.kt`).
- **Bedtime routine:** the M33 bedtime timer gains a volume (as it is, 80, 60 or 40 %) and starts
  by itself when the window opens while a book plays, not only on Play inside it. Once a night;
  the quieter volume lasts until the timer ends (`VolumeMixer.bedtime`). The window opening is
  `BedtimeAlarm`, an inexact `setAndAllowWhileIdle` alarm: no permission, works with the app
  closed while the service plays. The routine is the active profile's own: the app hands it over at
  start and on every profile switch (`apps/app/src/bedtime.ts`); a switch also ends the last
  listener's timer and its volume. Turning the routine off or changing its volume applies at once.
  A cast receiver keeps its own volume for now ([M125](m125.md)).
- **After the sleep timer:** when the timer stops playback the service keeps where and when, and
  where and when the listener last used the player (since [M138](m138.md), from the playback
  history). The player then offers “Back
  to when you last used it” with the clock time and how long before the stop it was (“23:41, 18 min
  before it stopped”), and 5, 10 or 15 min back. Nothing jumps by itself, and the app never says
  when someone fell asleep. Every control counts as use: anything through the session's player
  (app, notification, lock screen, headphones, watch, car), Play on a book from the library, the
  widget's skips, setting, changing or extending the timer, and a shake. Auto-advance, smart rewind and the timer's own stop don't.
  The offer goes once the listener plays on past the stop, after a day, or on a profile switch
  (`sleepStopChoices` in `packages/core/src/sleep.ts`, `SleepStopBanner.tsx`).
