# M77 Hide from library

A library folder can hold music next to the books. The listener hides such items, sees them under
Hidden and brings them back. The app suggests items that look like music, and never hides
anything by itself (see [Decisions](../brief.md#decisions)).

- **Hiding:** swipe a Library row to the left, or choose Hide in its long-press menu (the ⋯ button
  on the web). A bar says what was hidden and offers Undo for 8 s. TalkBack gets the same as a
  row action, “Hide”. A book that plays keeps playing.
- **Hidden view:** a “Hidden (N)” pill next to the sort pills, shown once something is hidden,
  lists only the hidden items. There a swipe, the row action or the menu says “Show in library”.
  With nothing left it says “Nothing hidden”.
- **Where hidden items don’t show:** every list reads books through `selectBooks` in
  `packages/core/src/library/queries.ts`, which leaves out hidden books unless asked
  (`HiddenFilter`: `visible`, `hidden`, `all`). So the Library, Continue listening (Home, the
  lock screen at start-up, the widget’s book after a restart), tag and smart lists, the badges on
  catalog tiles in Home and Search, and Android Auto’s shelves leave them out. Recommendation seeds
  leave them out as well. A book’s page, the player and imports still find them. Up Next is the
  listener’s own queue: a hidden book queued by hand stays there.
- **Data:** `hidden_books` (migration `0020`), per profile, keyed on the book’s location (its
  synced name, the same as tags and catalog links use), not its row ID, so a mark outlives a
  rescan, a regrouping and a book that comes back with a new row. `hidden` and `not_music` are
  separate: Undo clears `hidden` only; “Show in library” also sets `not_music`. Code:
  `packages/core/src/library/hidden.ts`.
- **Sync and export:** a `hidden` operation per profile and book, the newest change wins, like
  tags. The full export carries it, as it carries every synced change. A mark for a book that
  isn’t on a device yet waits there for the book.
- **Suggestions:** a card at the top of the Library: “N items look like music.” Review lists them,
  each ticked; “Hide selected” hides the ticked ones and keeps the rest as not music. The ✕ on the
  card keeps them all as not music. Not-music items are never suggested again, and can still be
  hidden by hand.
- **Scoring:** `musicScore` in `packages/core/src/library/music.ts`, a pure function with every
  number in `MUSIC_RULES`. An item needs 4 points from at least 2 signals:

  | Signal                                                                          | Points |
  | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -----: |
  | One file, under 8 minutes, no chapters                                          |      2 |
  | An ISRC tag                                                                     |      3 |
  | Else an ISRC-shaped code in the title or file name (`[A-Z]{2}[A-Z0-9]{3}\d{7}`) |      2 |
  | A music genre (a word list, or an ID3v1 number below 80)                        |      2 |
  | Track number, album and year, with no narrator or composer                      |      2 |
  | A FLAC file                                                                     |      1 |
  | A spoken genre: Audiobook, Audio Book, Speech, Spoken Word, Podcast and a few   |    −10 |

  Never suggested: several files, chapters, `.m4b`, MP4 `stik` audiobook or podcast, Jellyfin
  AudioBook items, Audiobookshelf, streamed books and podcast episodes. `musicSuggestions` in
  `packages/core/src/library/musicSuggestions.ts` scores single-file books the profile hasn’t
  decided on.

- **Tags:** `IdTags` in `modules/library` reads the ISRC (ID3 `TSRC` or `TXXX:ISRC`, MP4
  `----:com.apple.iTunes:ISRC`, FLAC `ISRC` comments), a narrator tag (`TXXX:NARRATOR`, `©nrt`,
  FLAC `NARRATOR`) and the MP4 media kind (`stik`); `TagReader` adds genre, track, year and
  composer from Android’s reader. They are stored as `book_files.kind_tags`. On Android, the
  first scan after the update reads single-file books once more for them. Jellyfin gives genre,
  track, year, narrator and the item type. The web and other servers give less: the score then
  rests on length, file name and title.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) Swipe a book in the Library to the left past a third of the row: it slides off over an
      amber “Hide”, leaves the list, and a bar says “Hidden: <title>” with Undo. Undo brings it
      back in place.
- [ ] (adb) Long-press a book and choose Hide: it leaves the list and “Hidden (1)” appears next
      to the sort pills. Tap it: only that book is listed. Swipe it left (“Show in library”): it
      returns to the Library and the view says “Nothing hidden”.
- [ ] (you) Swipe feel: a short swipe springs back, a quick flick hides, scrolling up and down the
      Library never hides a row, and a tap still opens the player.
- [ ] (you) [TalkBack] A Library row offers the action “Hide”; a row in Hidden offers “Show in
      library”. The bar’s text is read out when it appears, and Undo is reachable before it goes.
- [ ] (adb) Hide two books, pull down to rescan, then force-stop and reopen the app: both stay
      hidden and “Hidden (2)” is still there.
- [ ] (adb) Hide the book that is playing: playback goes on, the mini player stays, and the book
      leaves the Library and Continue listening on Home.
- [ ] (you) [widget] Hide the last-played book, close the app and restart the phone: the widget
      and lock screen don’t offer the hidden book on the next start.
- [ ] (you) [car] With a hidden book that was started, Android Auto’s Continue listening and On
      this phone shelves don’t list it.
- [ ] (adb) With a folder of single music files (a FLAC with an ISRC comment, an MP3 with `TSRC`,
      an M4A with `stik` 1) beside an `.m4b` and a multi-part book: after the scan the card says how
      many items look like music, the `.m4b` and the book are not among them.
- [ ] (adb) Review: untick one, Hide selected. The ticked ones are hidden, the bar offers Undo,
      the card is gone and the unticked one is not suggested after a rescan. The ✕ on the card
      does the same for all.
- [ ] (you) The maintainer’s library: the music files from the chosen folder are suggested, no
      audiobook is, and the first scan after the update (which reads single-file books again)
      takes an acceptable time.
- [ ] (you) [sync] Hide a book on the phone and sync: the web app paired with it hides it too.
      Bring it back on the web: after a sync it is back on the phone.
- [ ] (you) Settings → Export, then import on a fresh web app and add the same server: the hidden
      books stay hidden.
- [ ] (you) A Jellyfin music library: songs with a genre and year can be suggested; items of a
      books library never are.
- [ ] (you) Malay: a native speaker checks the 15 new lines in `ms.json` (Hide selected, Show in
      library, Nothing hidden and the rest).
