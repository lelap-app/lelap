# M22 Smarter audio

Volume boost, voice EQ and mono, on Android and the web. Skip silence and chapter guessing were
out of scope.

- **Android:** `SoundProcessor` runs `SoundChain` in ExoPlayer's `DefaultAudioSink`, in front of
  its own processors. Mono averages the channels; voice EQ is a high-pass at 80 Hz and a +4 dB peak
  at 3 kHz; the boost adds 6 dB with a peak limiter that keeps every sample under full scale.
  Switches ramp over 50 ms with no rebuffering. JVM unit tests cover the filters, mono, the
  limiter and the ramp.
- **Web:** a Web Audio graph in `apps/app/src/platform/webaudio.ts`. Web Audio can only read sources that allow CORS, so
  only downloads and hosts that send CORS headers get the effects; others play without them.
  Turning an effect on reloads the part once at the same place.
- **Setting:** in Player → Sound and Settings → Playback, for every book. Mono and voice EQ follow
  the listener and sync; volume boost stays with the device.

Done when the three switches sound right on headphones, the phone speaker and Bluetooth.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) Headphones: each switch while playing changes the sound within a moment, without a click,
      stall or rebuffer. Mono puts a hard-panned recording in both ears.
- [ ] (you) Speaker: volume boost is clearly louder on a quiet LibriVox recording and loud passages don't
      crackle. Voice clarity makes a muffled recording easier to follow without hiss.
- [ ] (you) Bluetooth: the same as on headphones and the speaker, also after reconnecting a headset
      mid-book.
- [ ] (you) The effects hold at 0.8× and 3×, and after a part changes on its own.
- [ ] (you) A phone call and a VoIP call mid-book duck or pause and resume; ducking and the sleep fade
      still work with boost on.
- [ ] (you) Casting plays on the receiver without these effects; the switches don't disturb a cast
      session, and local playback picks them up again afterwards.
- [ ] (you) An hour with all three effects on costs no noticeable extra battery.
- [ ] (you) Mono and voice clarity changed on another device reach the player at the next start (synced
      settings are applied at start, as with smart rewind); volume boost does not.
- [ ] (you) Web: a downloaded book and a LibriVox stream (archive.org sends CORS headers) play with the
      effects; a podcast host without CORS headers plays without them, and never in silence.
- [ ] (you) Web: turning a switch on while playing reloads the part once and continues at the same
      place.
- [ ] (you) Web, Safari and Firefox: the boost stage takes back the compressor's make-up gain as the spec
      defines it; levels match Chrome's.
