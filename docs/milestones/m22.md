# M22 Smarter audio

**Status:** in progress. Volume boost, voice EQ and mono through Media3's audio processors, and the
same three with Web Audio on the web. Skip silence and chapter guessing are out of scope.

- **Android:** `SoundProcessor` runs `SoundChain` in ExoPlayer's `DefaultAudioSink`, in front of its
  own processors (speed). Mono averages the channels; voice EQ is a high-pass at 80 Hz and a +4 dB
  peak at 3 kHz (biquads); the boost adds 6 dB and a peak limiter with instant attack keeps every
  sample under full scale. Switches ramp over 50 ms while playing, with no rebuffering. JVM unit
  tests cover the filter responses, mono, the limiter's ceiling and the ramp.
- **Web:** a `MediaElementAudioSourceNode` graph (`webaudio.ts`): a one-channel gain into a
  `ChannelMerger` for mono, two `BiquadFilterNode`s, a `GainNode` and a `DynamicsCompressorNode` as
  the limiter. Web Audio can only read sources that allow CORS, so the player routes an element
  only for downloads and hosts that send CORS headers; other sources play without the effects.
  Turning an effect on reloads the part once at the same place.
- **Setting:** one per listener, for every book, in Player → Sound and Settings → Playback. Mono and
  voice EQ suit the listener's ears and sync; volume boost suits a quiet phone speaker, so it stays
  with the device.

Device checks are in [`m22-follow-ups.md`](../m22-follow-ups.md).

**Done when** the three switches sound right on headphones, the phone speaker and Bluetooth.
