# M63 Playback fixes

Review findings in `modules/audio` and the app’s player that were left for later. What shipped:

- **Journal** (`PositionJournal`, `PlaybackService.writeJournal`): the place is written alone on
  every tick; the book has its own key and is written once per load, or when its speed, chapters
  or intro change. The speed is the live one, whichever controller changed it. The place carries
  `pausedAt`, so resuming from headphones measures smart rewind from the pause itself. The older
  journal, with the book inside the place, still reads.
- **Same book, new files** (`switchFiles`): loading the book that is loaded with other file
  addresses switches to them at the live place. A download that finishes, is removed or moves to
  another folder now reaches the player; before, the service kept the old files until another
  book was loaded. Removing a streamed book from the library empties the player.
- **Same book, new chapters**: chapters found from silences reach a playing book on Android
  (`updateChapters` in `apps/app/src/player.tsx`): the lock screen title follows them and a chapter
  timer moves to the new chapter’s end.
- **Profile switch**: emptying the player for a profile with nothing to play drops the journal’s
  book, so headphones and the car can’t resume the last listener’s book; its place stays for its
  own profile. A book that can’t load for the new profile empties the player too.
- **Errors reach JavaScript**: every custom session command answers its promise with the service’s
  result (`sendCommand` in `AudioModule`), so a book the service refuses rejects `load`.
- **Skip interval**: a new interval is announced to connected controllers (`SkipIncrements`), not
  only through the button icons.
- **Intro**: a place saved inside a show’s intro stays there; only a start from 0 skips the intro
  (`trimmedStart` in `@lelap/core` and `SeekRules`).
- **Up Next after a download move**: the app sends the queue and the car’s books again before old
  copies are deleted, and after a download is removed.
- **Accounting log** (`AccountingLog`): one listening total per book and day while nobody has
  taken it, and only a session’s latest progress event, so weeks with the app closed keep it small.
  `lelap_accounting` is left out of Android backups (`plugins/withBackupRules.js`).
- **Diagnostics**: a book ID longer than 200 characters drops the book from a player event, not the
  event.
- The spike screen labels `partTitle` as the chapter; `PlayerState.partTitle` says what it is.

Already fixed before this milestone: the paused load at start-up no longer moves `lastPlayedAt`
(the journal carries the time the place last moved, since M7), and a book played to its end is
marked finished (the service’s end-of-book log, folded by the app).
