# M105 App polish 3

What [M80](m80.md) left out. What shipped:

- **“New” badges for podcasts** (M49): the Podcasts tab draws a “3 new” badge on a show's cover
  for unstarted episodes that arrived since its page was last opened, and says it in the row's
  name. The podcast page marks those episodes “New” and draws the badge on its cover while it is
  open; opening it clears the tab's badge. “Arrived” is when a refresh first stored the episode
  on this device (`episodes.added_at`), since a feed's date is often older than the refresh that
  brings it. Episodes dated before counting started never count, so a show subscribed on another
  phone, or kept from before M105, doesn't light up with its back catalogue
  (`subscriptions.seen_at` and `new_since`). All three are kept per device, never synced, and
  leave `updatedAt` alone so they start no push; migration `0024_podcast_seen`
  (`markPodcastSeen`, `startSeenClocks`, `isNewEpisode` in
  `packages/core/src/podcasts/repository.ts`). The badge follows Settings → Badges on covers.
- **Covers by their first bytes** (M80): the proxy's `/cover` takes JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP and AVIF
  by their signature instead of the `Content-Type`, so artwork sent as
  `application/octet-stream` shows, and answers with the type the bytes name. SVG, HTML and
  audio are refused whatever their type says; an M4A's `ftyp` doesn't pass as AVIF. The limit is
  10 MB, for 3000 px podcast artwork. Every body is judged on its first 12 bytes, and the
  download stops there when they aren't an image, so audio sent as
  `application/octet-stream` is never read past its first bytes (`imageTypeOf` in `services/proxy/src/app.ts`).
- **A key for the zoomed scrubber** (M80): on the web, Z zooms the position bar in or out while
  it has focus, and ? lists it.
- **Saved covers fall back** (M80): a matched cover kept on the phone that has gone missing
  shows the catalog's address instead of the letter, and is saved again on the next library
  change (`catalogCoverSource` in `apps/app/src/catalogCovers.ts`). The player checks the file
  before handing it to the notification.
- **Subsonic and Plex chapter titles read again** (M67): a file an add-on gives a title for,
  stored without one (as the browser kept them before M67), is read once more on the next scan,
  so “001 …” becomes the chapter's title (`titled` in `filesNeedingTags`,
  `packages/core/src/library/scan.ts`).

Moved to [M118](m118.md), each with its reason: suggestion covers deeper in a page (a change to
the recommendations privacy rule), Plex libraries from a list (a protocol change), a Library that
reads only what changed (a design across every writer), Smart AudioBook Player bookmarks (no
sample or documented format).

      back: the badge is gone.
