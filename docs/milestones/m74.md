# M74 Car and widget 2

Android extras left out of M17, M25 and M33.

- Widget covers that need a server login show a placeholder; pass the app’s local copy of the cover to the widget. (M25)
- The widget’s Back and Forward do nothing while nothing is loaded. (M25)
- A `ContentProvider` for local covers in the car. (M17)
- A list of “this is my car” devices, for cars that report as a generic audio device. (M33)
- Refine the headphone battery estimate (20 % per hour assumed) with real numbers from earbuds. (M33)
- Car mode that looks good, not only big (the maintainer's wish, 2026-11-18): keep the large
  targets, and give it the app's own look, with the cover large, clear type for title and chapter,
  calm spacing and the amber accent, in both themes. Check it at arm's length on a phone in a
  holder, and against Android's driving guidelines (no small text, few choices per screen).

Done when a WebDAV book shows its cover in the widget and in Android Auto, and car mode is as
pleasant to look at as the player.
