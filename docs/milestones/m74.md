# M74 Car and widget 2

Android extras left out of M17, M25 and M33, and a car mode that looks like the player.

- **Car mode** (`apps/app/app/car.tsx`): the cover large, the title in 28 points and the chapter in
  amber in 20 below it, a thin amber bar for the place in the chapter, then back, play and forward
  as round buttons (96, 128 and 96 dp) and next chapter and sleep as two wide ones. No text under
  18 points and the same five choices as before. Night colours by default; with the phone in light
  mode it turns light for daylight (the rest of the app stays night). The cover takes the room
  the text leaves, so a headphone warning never pushes the buttons off. The app stays upright on
  phones; on wider screens (the web, tablets) the cover sits beside the controls.
- **Covers in Android Auto:** covers saved on the phone reach the car's lists through
  `CoverProvider` in `modules/audio`, a read-only provider at `<package>.covers`. The car gets a
  hash, never the path, and the provider only opens covers of books the car lists (`CarCovers`).
- **Widget Back and Forward with nothing loaded:** they go through the session's new `SKIP`
  command, which loads the last book paused at its place first, so they work after the app was
  closed or the phone restarted. With no book at all they are hidden.
- **Your cars:** with “Offer car mode in the car” on, Settings lists the connected Bluetooth audio
  devices and the marked ones; marking one counts it as a car for the offer and leaves it out of
  the headphone battery warning. Kept on this phone only.
- **Headphone battery:** the drain is measured while a book plays, between the level's drops,
  which come the moment a step is crossed, and remembered per device across sessions (half old,
  half new). A device that shows it lasts longer is no longer held to 20 % per hour; 8 % per hour
  is the floor, and a slow session counts for at most a quarter less than earlier ones
  (`packages/core/src/headphones.ts`).
- **Widget covers from servers:** already fine since M53, which saves a WebDAV, SMB, SFTP or S3
  folder's cover on the phone; the widget reads that copy. Audiobookshelf and Jellyfin send covers
  without a login.
