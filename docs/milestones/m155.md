# M155 Preparing in the player

What [M152](m152.md) and [M153](m153.md) left to the player. The decisions are in the brief under
[Preparing streams…](../brief.md#add-on-protocol) and [Stream choice in the app](../brief.md#add-on-protocol).

## What shipped

- **Preparing audio in the player.** Audio that answers `503` with `Retry-After` while it plays,
  marked or not, and a part in the middle of a book: on Android the load policy
  (`ExpiringLinkPolicy` in `PlaybackService.kt`, rules in `PreparingAudio.kt`) waits as long as
  the source says, for up to 10 minutes from its first answer, while every other failure keeps
  Media3's usual number of tries. The web player (`apps/app/src/platform/audio.web.ts`) probes
  the address after the element fails (`probeStream`, `preparingRetryAt` in
  `packages/core/src/addons/preparing.ts`) and opens the part again when told; `404` or `410`
  shows the source's words. `PlayerState.preparingUntil` drives “Preparing…” with a countdown
  under the player (`PreparingLine` in `Preparing.tsx`) instead of the error; after 10 minutes
  the line says the source is still getting the book ready.
- **Seeking past an unmeasured part.** A seek into a part nobody knows the length of is settled
  once the player opened it: a place past its end goes on into the parts after it
  (`pastPart` in `packages/core/src/library/timeline.ts`, `SeekRules.pastPart` and
  `settlePendingSeek` on Android), for the app's whole-book seeks and the skip buttons.
- **Chapters once measured.** A book of unmeasured parts keeps the meta's chapters and length in
  its add-on reference (`waitingChapters`, no migration); when the player measures the last part,
  `chaptersOnceMeasured` (`packages/core/src/addons/streamed.ts`) applies them if they fit, else
  one chapter per part, replacing chapters found from silences, and hands them to the loaded book.
- **The next copy of a release.** Play from an item page row arms a fallback (`playItem` in
  `apps/app/src/addons.ts`): when the player reports an error before the copy played, the app
  plays the row's next ready copy in M153's order (`nextCopy`, `copyFallbackStep` in
  `streamChoice.ts`), in the same book. A copy that fails after it played keeps its error.

Tests: `preparing.test.ts`, `timeline.test.ts`, `streamChoice.test.ts`, `streamed.test.ts`;
Kotlin `PreparingAudioTest`, `SeekRulesTest`, `PlayerErrorsTest`.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) A test add-on with an unmarked stream whose URL answers `503` with `Retry-After: 5`
      twice, then the audio: Play opens the player, which shows “Preparing…” with a countdown
      instead of an error, then plays on its own.
- [ ] (adb) A book of three parts whose second part answers `503` with `Retry-After: 10` once:
      at the end of part one the player shows “Preparing…”, then plays part two from its start.
- [ ] (adb) A part answering `503` with `Retry-After: 60` for more than 10 minutes: the player
      stops waiting and says the source is still getting the book ready.
- [ ] (adb) A book of four parts without `durationMs`, none opened: jump to a bookmark or shared
      place two parts in; playback starts at that place, not at the start of the next part.
- [ ] (adb) A book of parts without `durationMs` whose meta has chapters and a length that fits:
      once each part was opened, the chapters list shows the meta's chapters without adding the
      book again, and the lock screen names them.
- [ ] (adb) Two test add-ons offering the same `releaseId`, the first copy's URL answering `404`:
      Play plays the second copy, with no error left under the player, and the library has one
      book.
- [ ] (you) The web app with an Internet Archive item still being derived, on a server that sends
      CORS and `Access-Control-Expose-Headers: Retry-After`: the player shows “Preparing…” and
      plays once the file is there.
