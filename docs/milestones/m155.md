# M155 Preparing in the player

[M152](m152.md) handles preparing streams on the item page, before anything plays, and books of
unmeasured parts in the library. Left for the player:

- **Preparing audio the add-on didn't mark**, and a part that is still preparing in the middle
  of a book. Media3 and the web player meet the `503` with `Retry-After` themselves; show “Preparing…” in the player and try again when it says,
  as the item page does, instead of the error under the player.
- **Seeking past an unmeasured part** opens it first, so the seek lands where the listener meant
  instead of at the next part's start.
- **The meta's chapters once the last part is measured**, without adding the book again.
- **The next copy of a release when one fails** ([M153](m153.md)): when the player can't open a
  copy's audio, play the item page row's next ready copy, without adding a book per copy.
