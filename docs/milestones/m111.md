# M111 Podcasts 6

The Podcasting 2.0 tags [M75](m75.md) stored but didn't show.

- **Place.** The podcast page shows the show's `podcast:location` under its author, and an
  episode's own place under its line when it differs from the show's. A tap opens it in the
  phone's map app through a `geo:` link; in a browser, or on a phone without a map app, it opens
  the place on openstreetmap.org: the OpenStreetMap object when the feed names one, else the point,
  else a search for the name. Lelap draws no map, so no tiles come from our servers (`mapLink` and
  `geoPoint` in `packages/core/src/podcasts/podcasting.ts`, `PlaceLink` in
  `apps/app/app/podcast.tsx`).
- **Value for value.** A show with `podcast:value` gets a card on its page: that it takes small
  payments as you listen, through what (Lightning, Hive, Web Monetization), who gets which share,
  and that Lelap does not send them. Fee recipients take their split off the top; the rest is
  shared by split (`valueInfoOf`). A show whose feed names recipients only per episode shows the
  newest such episode's. Recipients now keep `fee`; rows stored earlier read as no fee until the
  feed is read again.

Sending payments is not part of this milestone: it would move money and needs a decision first.
