# M150 Better sound 5

What [M134](m134.md) left out.

- **VBR MP3 in Firefox and Safari.** Firefox 147 seeks by the Xing table too, but then
  estimates the frame from an average frame length, 1 to 3 frames off in ways that varied by
  file. Following it needs its rule in full (how it averages, from which frames), measured with
  M134's harness on more files. Safari wasn't measured.
- **Where an MP3 seek lands.** After a seek in a constant-bitrate MP3, Chrome played 21 to 50 ms
  behind the frames' times and Firefox one frame ahead. Placing the map by each browser's seek
  would take up to two frames off the fast stretches' edges.
- **Time saved while casting.** When the phone renders a part for a receiver, skip silence's
  time saved isn't added to the book's count; the stream's skipped frames could be.
- **Joining a rendered group.** A speaker that joins while the phone renders makes the whole
  group start the stream again; the newcomer could get its own stream from the leader's place.
