# M120 Big screens 3

Builds on [M113](m113.md). The decision is in the brief under [Big screens](../brief.md#decisions).

- **Drag on Android:** on a tablet or another wide window, press a book row or an episode, hold it
  a moment (250 to 500 ms) and move it onto a list; a finger, a stylus and a mouse all work. A
  quick move still scrolls, and holding still opens the row's menu as before. It is React Native's
  own responder system, no gesture library: `apps/app/src/drag.tsx` draws the item's title under
  the pointer (`DragHost`) and measures the lists as the drag starts. `dragStarts` and `zoneAt` in
  `packages/core/src/screen.ts` decide when it starts and where it lands. Phones keep the menu.
- **Drag from catalog tiles:** a tile on Home or Discover and a Search result can be dragged too,
  on the web and on Android (`DragWork` in `MetaCard.tsx`). The drop adds it to the library first,
  as Play does but without playing: your own copy, else the source and edition chosen on its page,
  else the first source (`addWork` in `apps/app/src/addons.ts`). A show is followed from its page,
  so a podcast tile doesn't drag.
- **Drop on the Library's list cards** as well as on the rail (`ListCard` in
  `app/(tabs)/library.tsx`); both use `useListDrop` (`apps/app/src/listDrop.ts`). A card lights
  up while a drag is over it and says Added; a smart list takes no drop.
- **Insets around a hinge:** each side of a hinge gets only the window's insets on its own edges
  (`insetsBeside`, `HingeSide.tsx`), so the screens above a tabletop's hinge leave no room for the
  navigation bar, and the player below it none for the status bar.
- **Hinge and window in step:** `modules/fold` sends the window's size with the hinge, and
  `foldLayout` lays out from both, so a hinge that arrives a frame before React Native's new window
  size no longer splits the old window.
- **One page at a time:** a book's or a show's page open full screen moves into the middle column
  when the window widens to three columns (`DetailPane.tsx`), and a page another profile opened
  closes on a profile switch (`navigation.ts`).

Checked in the web app at 768, 1280 and 1920 px: a catalog tile dropped on the rail's list adds
the book to the library and the list; a Library row dropped on a list card adds it; a page open
full screen at 1280 px moves into the column at 1920 px.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) [drag] A 10-inch tablet sideways with a hand-made list: in the Library, press a book,
      hold it about a third of a second and move it up and down and onto the list in the rail; the
      title follows the finger, the list lights up, and after letting go it says Added and has the
      book. Repeat with a quick vertical move: the list scrolls instead.
- [ ] (adb) [drag] The same tablet: hold a row still for a second; the row's menu opens and no drag
      starts. Swipe a row to the left; it still hides as before.
- [ ] (adb) [drag] The same tablet: drag an episode from a show's page and a tile from Home onto
      the list in the rail and onto a list card at the top of the Library. The tile's book joins
      the library and the list; a smart list doesn't light up.
- [ ] (you) [drag] A tablet with a stylus, and one with a mouse (or a Chromebook): hold and drag a
      book onto a list. It works with both, and a quick mouse drag scrolls the list.
- [ ] (adb) A phone: holding and moving a row in the Library scrolls the list as before; no title
      follows the finger.
- [ ] (adb) [fold] A foldable half open like a tabletop: the tabs above the hinge sit right on it
      with no empty band for the navigation bar; the player below keeps its room for the navigation
      bar and none for the status bar.
- [ ] (adb) [fold] The same foldable half open like a book: the screens' side has no gap at the
      hinge, and the player's side keeps the window's right-hand inset only.
- [ ] (adb) [fold] Fold and unfold quickly ten times on the foldable emulator: no frame shows the
      player cut off or a pane crossing the hinge.
- [ ] (adb) A tablet upright with a book's page open full screen from Home: turn it to landscape at
      1400 dp wide or more; the page moves into the middle column and Back closes it without
      leaving Home.
- [ ] (adb) The same tablet in landscape with a page in the middle column: switch to another
      profile; the column closes.
- [ ] (you) In Chrome, Firefox and Safari at 1920 px: drag a tile from Home and a Search result
      with the mouse onto a list in the rail and onto a list card in the Library; each joins the
      library and the list.
