# M51 Background accounting

**Status:** in progress. On Android the playback service counts listening time and scrobble sessions for everything it plays, app open or closed, and the app merges them into Stats and the scrobble outbox once each when it wakes. Phone checks are in [`m51-follow-ups.md`](../m51-follow-ups.md).

What the playback service hears while the app is swiped away counts too: listening time for Stats ([M24](m24.md)) and progress for scrobble add-ons ([M30](m30.md)), tallied in the service and handed to JavaScript when the app wakes.

## Done when

- A night of listening with the app swiped away shows in Stats and reaches Hardcover the next morning.
