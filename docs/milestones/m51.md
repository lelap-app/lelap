# M51 Background accounting

On Android the playback service counts listening time and scrobble sessions for everything it
plays, app open or closed (`ListeningTally` and `AccountingLog` in `modules/audio`), and the app
merges them into Stats and the scrobble outbox exactly once when it wakes
(`mergeServiceAccounting` in `packages/core`). The app no longer counts on Android
(`countsInService` in `@lelap/audio`), so nothing is counted twice; the web keeps counting in the
tab. Time comes from the elapsed clock, so changing the phone's clock moves nothing, and process
death loses at most the last 10 s.

Done when a night of listening with the app swiped away shows in Stats and reaches Hardcover the
next morning.
