# M21 Translations

**Status:** in progress (built in [#81](https://github.com/lelap-app/lelap/pull/81)). The app in English
and Bahasa Melayu, chosen per profile. Open: the “Done when” checks on a phone, and a native
speaker's review of the Malay.

## What it has

- `apps/app/src/i18n`: `t()` with the English text as key and `{holes}` for values; Malay in
  `ms.ts`, falling back to English line by line.
- Settings → Language, stored as the profile's `language` setting. English by default; a new
  profile starts with its creator's language and can change it.
- Switching the language, or to a profile with another language, redraws the app.
- `pnpm --filter @lelap/app test` checks the catalog against the code.
- `src/i18n/README.md` for translators.

## Not yet

- Native Android text (notification channels, the widget, the Quick Settings tile) and the
  landing page stay English.
- Dates and numbers follow the device, not the profile's language.
- The Malay needs a review by a native speaker.

## Done when

- Every screen reads in Malay with no English left, except brand names.
- A kids profile in Malay and a grown-up in English on one phone each keep their language when
  switching.
