# M60 Internet Archive

A first-party hosted add-on on the catalog Worker at `/internet-archive`
(`services/catalog/src/internet-archive`), next to the LibriVox add-on: old-time radio as podcasts
with a feed per series, and lectures and readings as audiobooks, through archive.org's open API.
Listeners add it by its manifest URL. It serves only items whose archive.org rights fields say
public domain or a Creative Commons licence (see Internet Archive in the
[brief](../brief.md#decisions)); audio streams from archive.org, never through Lelap's servers.

Done when searching for an old radio show finds it and its episodes play from archive.org.

      `application/rss+xml`. The catalog Docker image answers `/internet-archive/manifest.json` and a
      feed too.
