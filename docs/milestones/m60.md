# M60 Internet Archive

**Status:** in progress. The add-on runs on the catalog Worker at `/internet-archive`: old-time
radio as podcasts with a feed of each series, lectures and readings as audiobooks, only items in
the public domain or under a Creative Commons licence. Phone checks are in
[m60-follow-ups.md](../m60-follow-ups.md).

A first-party hosted add-on for public-domain audio on archive.org beyond LibriVox: old-time
radio, lectures and other volunteer recordings, through archive.org's open API. It runs next to
the LibriVox add-on and reuses its code; listeners add it by its manifest URL. Audio streams from
archive.org, never through Lelap's servers.

## Done when

- Searching for an old radio show finds it, and its episodes play from archive.org.
