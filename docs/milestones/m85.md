# M85 Chapters and bookmarks 2

## Find chapters starts at once

The fix for M64's open check. Over adb, the silence job's constraints were
`BATTERY_NOT_LOW FLEXIBILITY`: Android's flexible scheduling held it back at 84 %, and the sheet
said “Waiting for enough battery.”. A scan the listener asks for now starts at once:

- `Silences.enqueue` queues expedited work (`RUN_AS_NON_EXPEDITED_WORK_REQUEST` when the app is out
  of quota) with no constraints. The worker checks the battery itself (`Battery.kt`: not while
  charging, else Android's own low-battery flag, else 15 % or less): low at the start, or while it
  runs (every 5 s), it writes a `low-battery` marker, stops at its last checkpoint and retries 10
  minutes later.
- The worker runs in the foreground with a notification, “Finding chapters in {title}”, with a bar
  for how far it is through the book; `getForegroundInfo` covers expedited work before Android 12.
- The sheet says “Waiting to start.” unless the battery is low now, then “Waiting for enough
  battery.”. While it runs it shows a bar, no percentages. How far it is comes from
  `SilenceJob.fractionDone`: by the files' stored lengths, which the job now carries with the
  title, or by whole files when a length is unknown.

## Edit chapters

Any book's chapters can be fixed by hand: Chapters → Edit opens `app/chapters.tsx`, where each
chapter has its name and start (a typed time, or Now for the playing place), and chapters can be
added and removed. Saving cleans the list (`cleanChapters`: in order, inside the book, never two at
one start, an empty name becomes “Chapter n”) and stores it as the listener's own chapters.

- `own_chapters` (migration `0023`): per profile, kept by the book's synced name like hidden books,
  so a rescan that rebuilds the book never touches them; the book's own chapters (found ones too)
  stay underneath. `getBook`, `chapterTitleAt` and so the player, lock screen, chapter timer and
  Continue card use them (`packages/core/src/library/ownChapters.ts`).
- Synced and exported as a `chapters` change; Reset in the chapters sheet stores null, so the reset
  syncs too. Older devices skip the change.
- The Chapters button now shows for every book, so one without chapters can get them by hand.

## Bookmarks

- A bookmark's note can be changed at any time: long press it (or the TalkBack action) for its note,
  Share and Delete.
- Bookmarks in the Library's header lists every book's bookmarks, newest first, with the book and
  chapter (`listAllBookmarks`); a tap plays the book from there (`playAt`).

## Share a place

The bookmarks sheet shares a link to the place playing now, or to the last 30 seconds as a short
clip; a bookmark shares its own place. The link goes to the web app's `/place` (`webBase`,
`EXPO_PUBLIC_LELAP_WEB`) and names the book by title, author, ISBN or ASIN and catalog work, never a
file, folder or the audio (`packages/core/src/placeLink.ts`). Opening it finds the listener's own
copy on their device (`findPlaceBook`) and offers Play from the time; without a copy it says so and
offers the work in the catalog. In a phone's browser it also offers Open in the Lelap app
(`lelap://place?…`). See “Shared places” in the [brief](../brief.md).

Left for later: places in podcast episodes and clips that stop at their end ([M88](m88.md)), and
https links that open the app directly ([M90](m90.md)).
