# M56 Your copy, linked

A work's page in Discover or Search finds the same book in the listener's own library (a phone
folder, WebDAV, S3, Jellyfin or Audiobookshelf) on the device, with no request: the same ISBN or
ASIN in the tags or the server's metadata links on its own, and the same title and author after
normalising is offered as “In your library?” for one tap. A confirmed link is remembered and syncs
keyed on the book's location (`packages/core/src/library/links.ts`); the page lists “Your copy”
first and plays it. Match with catalog… links a book by hand and “Unlink” undoes it. The normalising
and scoring live in the SDK (`packages/sdk/src/match.ts`, MIT), used by the app and by source
add-ons; the sample is in `examples/source-addon` and the developer docs have a page on mapping
files to works.

Done when a WebDAV book under an unlike folder name shows as “In your library” after one
confirmation and plays, a book with an ISBN or ASIN tag links without asking, and a book that
matches nothing can be linked by hand.

## Open questions

- The work page always starts on “Your copy”, even when the listener picked an add-on's source for
  that work last time. This follows “your copy first”; should the remembered choice win instead?
