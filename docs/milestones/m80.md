# M80 App polish 2

The bigger items [M71](m71.md) left out, plus two found on a phone and in the web preview. What
shipped:

- **Zoomed scrubber** (M39): a long press on the elapsed time zooms the bar to ten minutes around
  the playhead (`zoomWindow` in `packages/core/src/seek.ts`, `Scrubber.tsx`). Marks, snapping, fine
  scrubbing, keys and TalkBack's swipes work inside the zoomed part; the times stay those of the
  whole range. Playing past either end moves the zoomed part along. A note over the bar says what
  it shows; tapping it, or another long press, zooms out. A range of ten minutes or less has
  nothing to zoom.
- **Matched covers offline** (M56): after Match with catalog… the phone keeps a copy of the
  work's cover in `catalog-covers/`, named by the SHA-256 of its address (`catalogCoverName` in
  `packages/core/src/library/links.ts`, `apps/app/src/catalogCovers.ts`). The link syncs, the copy
  doesn't: each phone saves its own after a match or a sync, keeps it only when it starts like an
  image (`looksLikeImage`), and removes copies no book uses. The folder is left out of Android
  backups. The web shows the address as before, kept by the browser's cache. A server folder's
  cover (`scan.ts`) now waits for its file to be moved into place before it is used.
- **Faster web saves** (M67): a save compares each 256 KB chunk with a copy of the last save kept
  in memory instead of hashing it (`ChunkedFile` in `packages/core/src/chunkedFile.ts`). No
  SHA-256 runs any more, in JavaScript or `crypto.subtle`. Loading reads the chunks without hashing
  them too.
- **Player tools on narrow screens**: the tools under the controls (speed, Sound, sleep timer,
  Chapters, Transcript, Soundbites, bookmarks, Cast) wrap into rows of equal count when they don't
  fit at 76 points each (more with large text), and labels wrap to a second line instead of
  ending in “…”. At 320 dp seven tools take three rows of three, three and one; at 360 dp and
  up, two rows. The five playback controls shrink to 48 points (play to 72) below 304 points, so
  ⏩ is no longer cut off at 320 dp. TalkBack labels are unchanged.
- **Blank episode cover on the web**: a cover that doesn't load showed an empty tile, without the
  title's letter. Reproduced in the web preview with an episode whose cover address answers 404.
  Now a cover that fails falls back to the letter tile, and a podcast chapter's picture that fails
  falls back to the episode's cover (`BookCover.tsx`). The same happened on Android. Covers over
  plain http go through the proxy, which refuses images over 5 MB or not sent as JPEG, PNG, WebP,
  GIF or AVIF; such a cover now shows the letter. Accepting those is in [M105](m105.md).

Moved to [M105](m105.md): suggestion covers deeper in a page (M46), “new” badges for podcasts
(M49), Plex libraries from a list (M59), Subsonic and Plex chapters read again (M67), reading only
changed books in the Library (M43), Smart AudioBook Player bookmarks (M34).
