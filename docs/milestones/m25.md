# M25 Home screen and tiles

**Status:** in progress. A home screen widget (4x1 and 4x2) with
cover, title, chapter, play or pause and back and forward by the skip interval; a Quick Settings
tile that plays or pauses the current book, with a long press opening the player; and a sleep timer
button on the media notification and lock screen that steps 15 min → 30 min → end of chapter →
off. All Android only, in `modules/widget` plus a small change to `modules/audio`, with no
JavaScript and no Google Play services, so they work with the app closed and in the F-Droid build.
Phone checks are in [`m25-follow-ups.md`](../m25-follow-ups.md).

**Done when** the widget, the tile and the notification's sleep button work on the phone with the
app swiped away.
