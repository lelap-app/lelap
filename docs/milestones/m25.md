# M25 Home screen and tiles

All Android only, with no JavaScript and no Google Play services, so they work with the app closed
and in the F-Droid build:

- **Widget** (`modules/widget`, `PlayerWidgetProvider`): 4x1 and 4x2 layouts with cover, title,
  chapter, play or pause, and back and forward by the skip interval. Covers come from their own
  host, never through our services, shrunk to 320 px and cached.
- **Quick Settings tile** (`PlayTileService`): plays or pauses the current book; a long press opens
  the player through `lelap://player` (a fork with another scheme changes `Links.kt` too).
- **Sleep button** on the media notification and lock screen (`SleepCycle` in `modules/audio`):
  15 min → 30 min → end of chapter → off.

`PlaybackService` saves what plays now (`NowPlaying`) for the widget and tile, so they show the
last book with the app closed; with nothing loaded, Play resumes it from the position journal.

Done when the widget, the tile and the notification's sleep button work on the phone with the app
swiped away.
