# M17 Car and offline web

Android Auto browses Continue listening, On this phone, Podcasts and Up Next from a snapshot the
app hands the playback service (`packages/core/src/car.ts`, the browse tree in `modules/audio`),
so the car works with the app closed. Local covers reach the car through a `ContentProvider`
since [M74](m74.md), as Android Auto can't open the app's files. A service worker keeps the web app's shell so the
web app starts offline; it never caches audio.

Done when the car checks pass on a head unit or the Desktop Head Unit.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) With “Unknown sources” on in Android Auto's developer settings, Lelap appears among the media
      apps (DHU or a car), and the root shows four tabs: Continue listening, On this phone, Podcasts,
      Up Next.
- [ ] (you) With the app swiped away, and after a reboot with the app not opened since, the Auto lists
      still load.
- [ ] (you) Picking a book in the car plays it from its saved place, with smart rewind; picking the book
      that is already loaded keeps its live place.
- [ ] (you) Picking an episode in the car, then letting it end, hands over to the next Up Next item.
- [ ] (you) Play book A in the car, then book B, then open the app: A's place is the one reached in the
      car (folded from the service's earlier-places list), and B's too.
- [ ] (you) Changing the library in the app (download a book, subscribe) updates the open car lists
      without reconnecting (`notifyChildrenChanged`).
- [ ] (you) http(s) covers show in the car; local covers too, through the provider (M74).
- [ ] (you) Streamed books whose links are signed (S3) still play from the car hours after the app last
      wrote the snapshot; if not, re-sign in the service or drop those books from the car lists.
- [ ] (you) Offline web: in the web app, after one visit, DevTools → Network → Offline, reload: the app
      starts and shows the library.
- [ ] (you) Deploy a new web build: the open tab keeps the old version and the new worker shows as
      “waiting”; after closing every tab and opening the app again the new version runs and the old
      `lelap-shell-*` cache is gone.
- [ ] (you) Streaming and downloading audio work in the web app with the worker in place, and no audio
      shows up in Application → Cache Storage.
- [ ] (you) Discover in the web app shows the last catalog answers when offline.
- [ ] (you) The Docker image (Caddy) serves `sw.js` with `Cache-Control: no-cache`, and the app starts
      offline there too.
