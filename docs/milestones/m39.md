# M39 Precise seeking

**Status:** planned (idea from 2026-11-17, not started).

Hitting the right minute in a 10-hour book is hard. The scrubber already covers the current chapter
instead of the whole book, which helps when chapters are short; long chapters and books without
chapters still need more.

Ideas to pick from:

- **Bookmarks on the bar:** small marks for bookmarks (and chapter starts when the bar shows the
  whole book); dragging near one snaps to it, with a light vibration on Android.
- **Fine scrubbing:** the further the finger moves up from the bar while dragging, the slower the
  playhead moves (half, quarter, fine), with the speed shown above the thumb.
- **Zoom:** switch the bar between chapter and whole book; a long press on the time opens a
  magnified bar around the playhead (±5 minutes).
- **Jump to a time:** tap the elapsed time to type `2:14:30`, in the book or the chapter.
- **Step buttons:** ±1 min and ±5 min in the seek sheet, next to the 30-second skips.

Done when finding a moment you remember (“about 2 h 14 in”) takes one or two moves.
