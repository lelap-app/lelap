# M39 Precise seeking

The scrubber (`apps/app/src/components/Scrubber.tsx`) shows bookmark dots and chapter ticks and
snaps to them (a short buzz on Android). Moving the finger away from the bar slows the playhead to
half, quarter and fine speed. A tap on the note under the bar switches between chapter and whole
book, and a tap on the elapsed time opens “Jump to”: a typed time such as `2:14:30`, `14:30` or
`90`, and ±1/±5 min. Helpers in `packages/core/src/seek.ts`.
