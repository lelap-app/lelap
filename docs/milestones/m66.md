# M66 Add-on protocol 3

Protocol and SDK work, and fixes in the first-party add-ons, left out of earlier milestones. New fields stay optional, as with every revision.

- A list of editions on `Meta` (today only `year`), so the item page can show and choose editions. (M15)
- Settings reach only `library` calls; an add-on’s catalogs and streams run without them. (M57)
- A library listing is one answer of at most 5 MB (about 20,000 files); paging needs a revision. (M57)
- The app sends no `abandon` or `rate`: there is no rating or “stop reading” control. (M30)
- `finish` events on Android carry no `sessionStartMs`; the service could add it to its end-of-book log. (M51)
- The tracker sends `start` on every resume, so each resume signs in to Open Library once, and resuming a finished book moves it back to Currently reading (Hardcover too). (M61)
- Books on your own storage linked to a Catalog work (M56) are scrobbled without the work’s ID; sending it as `externalIds.ol` covers them and gives Hardcover a better match. (M61)
- “Mark as finished” in the book menu doesn’t scrobble, to the reading log or Hardcover. (M61)
- `Retry-After` from Open Library is not passed on; a 429 waits on the outbox’s own backoff. (M61)
- The reading log’s User-Agent has no contact address, which Open Library suggests for heavy clients (the add-on keeps to 3 requests a second). (M61)
- The first-party scrobble add-ons seal their own settings; they could move to the SDK’s `configure` once it covers their OAuth flows. (M27)
- Audnexus is asked for the `us` region only. (M32)
- Look up Goodreads books missing from the library through the Catalog add-on, for a Want to read list. (M34)
- The LibriVox catalog rates from archive.org’s `avg_rating` and the item page from the item’s reviews; they can differ by a few tenths. (M49)
- Separately uploaded copies of one recording (128 kb and 64 kb) show as two recordings; a match on the name stem would merge them. (M60)
- Broadcast dates in file names are read as 19yy, so a recreation from the 2000s is dated a century early. (M60)
- One hidden WebView per add-on server while scanning; close a server’s sandbox after its scan to save memory. (M57)
- `stream_headers` rows can be left behind if foreign keys are off on a device. (M57)

Done when the item page can choose an edition and a third-party add-on gets its settings on every route.
