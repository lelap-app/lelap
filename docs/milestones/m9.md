# M9 Profiles

Profiles inside one household, with no accounts: a name, one of the bundled avatars or the name's
initial on a colour, an optional four-digit PIN (a SHA-256 hash with the profile ID; the UI says
it encrypts nothing) and a kids switch. “Who's listening?” shows at launch when there is more
than one profile. Per profile: places, finished and speed, bookmarks, settings, podcast
subscriptions, Up Next and which add-ons it sees; books, downloads and installed add-ons are
shared. A kids profile can't change add-ons or manage profiles. Profiles sync with
[M7](m7.md); the active profile is per device. Switching to a profile with nothing to play
empties the player (`unload`).

Done when two people in one household listen on the same phone and on their own devices, each
with their own progress, without signing in anywhere.
