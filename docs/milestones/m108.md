# M108 Themes

The start of the "Themes and community looks" goal, built on the night theme and the colours of
the M9 profile tiles. Settings → Theme, per profile like the language.

- **Themes:** Night (as before, the default), Dusk (a softer blue-grey dark), Day (the paper and
  ink of car mode by day), Sepia, High contrast, and “Like the phone”, which is Day when the phone
  is light and Night when it is dark. The colours live by role in `packages/core/src/themes.ts`;
  the role names stay the night theme's (`night` is the background, `paper` the text), so every
  class in the app is unchanged.
- **Accent:** Amber, Teal, Rose, Blue, Green or Violet, from the profile tiles' colours. Each has a
  bright shade for dark themes, a deep one for light themes and a brighter one for high contrast.
- **High contrast:** text at 7:1 or more, borders at 3:1 on the background, a 4 px focus ring on
  the web, no source badge drawn see-through over a cover and no blurred cover behind car mode.
- **Themes as a file:** “Save as a file” writes the colours on screen as a small JSON file
  (`lelap: "theme"`, `version: 1`, a name, light or dark, ten named colours); “Open a theme file”
  reads one back. The zod schema is strict, so a file with any other key (a font, a picture, code)
  is refused, and so is one whose colours fail a contrast pair; the reasons are shown. The last
  file read is kept in the settings and shows as its own theme.
- **How it draws:** Tailwind's colours are CSS variables (`tailwind.config.js`, defaults in
  `global.css`), set by `apps/app/src/theme.tsx` on the page (web) or through NativeWind's `vars()`
  on the root view (Android). Icons and other colours outside a class read `COLORS`, changed in
  place; a new theme draws the screens again under a new key, as a new language does.
- **Car mode and stats:** car mode keeps its own light by day and dark at night (the day theme by
  day, the listener's theme at night when it is dark, else night), always in the chosen accent.
  The stats and the year overview now follow the app's theme instead of the phone's.
- **Checks:** `themeProblems` lists every colour pair the app draws (text on the background and
  cards, text on the accent, text on a chosen row, outlines, car mode's and the stats' palettes).
  `pnpm test` runs it for every theme with every accent in Vitest and in
  `apps/app/scripts/check-accessibility.mjs`, which also fails on a theme colour written as a hex
  (it would not follow the theme), on `COLORS` read outside a component, and when
  `tailwind.config.js` or `global.css` drift from the themes. Decision in the
  [brief](../brief.md) (Themes).

Community skins and players stay a future goal.
