# M108 Themes

The start of the "Themes and community looks" goal. The M9 avatars and the night theme are what
exists today.

- **Themes:** a few built-in themes beyond light, dark and night, each checked by the contrast
  tests for every colour pair the app uses.
- **Accent colour:** let the listener pick the accent (amber today) from a short list that passes
  contrast on every theme.
- **High contrast:** a theme for low vision that builds on [M83](m83.md): stronger borders,
  larger focus rings, no text on images.
- **Themes as a file:** export and import a theme as a small JSON file, validated with zod, so
  people can share looks without an add-on. Colours only; no code, fonts or remote images.
- Community players stay a future goal.
