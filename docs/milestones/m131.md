# M131 Lepak

“Lepak” is Malay slang for hanging out. This add-on gathers free listening in Malay and Indonesian,
and from Singapore: podcasts from Podcast Index by language, and books in these languages from
open sources. One of Lelap's own add-ons, off by default:
[Lelap's own add-ons](../brief.md#decisions) in the brief.

**Where it runs: on the catalog service**, next to the Catalog
(`services/catalog/src/lepak/`, `/lepak/manifest.json`, `app.lelap.lepak`). Podcast Index needs an
API key, which the catalog service already holds (`podcastindex.ts`); the hand-kept book list and
one shared cache live there too.

## What the sources offer

Checked on 2026-11-19.

- **Podcast Index: a language filter on three endpoints.** `lang` takes one or more codes
  separated by commas, plus `unknown`, and is accepted by `podcasts/trending`, `recent/feeds` and
  `episodes/random`
  ([`lang.yaml`](https://github.com/Podcastindex-org/docs-api/blob/caf2697be05746b47200bb8cc1ea93d1ec3d7c3d/api_src/components/parameters/lang.yaml)
  and the paths that use it). `cat` narrows by category name or ID
  ([`cat.yaml`](https://github.com/Podcastindex-org/docs-api/blob/caf2697be05746b47200bb8cc1ea93d1ec3d7c3d/api_src/components/parameters/cat.yaml));
  `categories/list` names them. `search/byterm` has no `lang`, but each feed in its answer has a
  `language`, so search results are filtered after. The language comes from the feed's own
  `<language>`; whether `lang=ms` also matches `ms-MY`, and `en-sg` matches feeds that say `en-SG`,
  is not documented and is measured first (see Plan).
- **LibriVox: almost nothing.** archive.org's copy of the LibriVox catalog (`collection:librivoxaudio`)
  has 1 recording in Indonesian, none in Malay, 25 in Chinese and 2 in Tamil.
- **Internet Archive: plenty, but mostly not books.** Audio items in Malay or Indonesian that pass
  the [Internet Archive](../brief.md#decisions) rule (a Creative Commons licence or public domain):
  4,868. A sample is mostly recorded sermons, radio station captures and personal uploads, so a
  search can't make a books row.
- **Open Library: metadata only.** It knows works in Malay and Indonesian, but holds no audio, so it
  can only name and cover books whose audio comes from elsewhere.

## Plan

- **Measure the podcast side first.** With the catalog service's key, count what `podcasts/trending`
  and `recent/feeds` return for `ms`, `id`, `ms-my`, `id-id`, `en-sg`, `zh-sg`, `ms-sg` and `ta-sg`,
  and what feeds say in `language`. Write the numbers into this file. If region codes don't match,
  ask for the base language and keep feeds whose `language` ends in `-SG`.
- **Podcast rows**, each a catalog of `podcast` items with `lang=ms,id` (and the Singapore codes
  where they match), by categories whose exact names come from `categories/list`:
  - **Cerita**: stories and drama (fiction and drama categories);
  - **Sembang**: talk (society, culture, comedy and leisure categories);
  - **Sejarah**: history;
  - **Singapura**: shows that say `en-SG`, `zh-SG`, `ms-SG` or `ta-SG`. Feeds that only say `en`
    or `zh` can't be told apart from any other English or Chinese show, so they are left out.
  - Search over all of them, filtered by `language`.
- **Books, from a hand-kept list.** `services/catalog/src/lepak/books.ts`: archive.org identifiers
  and LibriVox projects in Malay, Indonesian, and Singapore's Chinese and Tamil where they exist,
  each checked against `internet-archive/rights.ts` when the list is built and again by a test.
  Streams come from the existing LibriVox and Internet Archive code; Open Library adds covers and
  authors where the work matches. The Cerita and Sejarah rows show these books before the
  podcasts. Until the list has at least a row's worth, the book rows stay out of the manifest.
- **Names.** The add-on and its rows carry Malay names, as Lelap's own name is Indonesian; the
  manifest's descriptions are in English, and `ms.json` has the Malay lines for the app's own
  text about the add-on.
- **Caching and limits.** Through `upstream.json`, rows for a day, as the Catalog's trending row
  is; no new Podcast Index calls per listener.
- **Offered, off by default**, with Add under Lelap's own add-ons, as BookBrainz is
  ([M129](m129.md)).
- **Tests.** Recorded Podcast Index answers for each row, the language filter on search, and the
  book list against the rights rule.

## Done when

- With Lepak added, Home shows Cerita, Sembang and Sejarah with Malay and Indonesian shows, and
  each subscribes like any podcast.
- Singapura lists shows whose feed says a Singapore language tag, or the row is left out with the
  measured numbers written here saying why.
- Every book on the list plays from LibriVox or archive.org and passes the rights rule in a test.
- Searching “sejarah” finds Malay and Indonesian shows only.
