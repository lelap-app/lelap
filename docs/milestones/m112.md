# M112 Lists 2

[M16](m16.md) made hand-made and smart lists that sync and survive an export. This milestone makes
them something to listen through. How it plays is a decision in the
[brief](../brief.md#decisions) (“Playing a list”).

- **Play a list:** a list's Play list button starts where the list stands and follows it: every
  item hands over to the next when it ends, books and episodes alike. Tapping an item plays the
  list from there. The playback service already handed episodes over to Up Next with the app
  closed; while a list plays, everything hands over, and the queue the app sends is Up Next
  first, then the rest of the list (`apps/app/src/player.tsx`: `playList`, `sendQueue`).
- **Order:** a hand-made list plays in its own order, so moving an item changes what comes next. A
  smart list plays in the order it had when Play was pressed (`ListFollow.order`), since playing
  an item reorders it or takes it out of a rule such as “Not started”.
- **Where you are:** the list marks what is playing and what is next. The player says which list
  it plays from, with what comes next, Previous and Next in list, and a button to stop following it
  (`ListBanner`). Playing a book outside the list and Up Next, from the app or the car, leaves the
  list; what plays then stops at its end like any book.
- **Finished items:** skipped by default. “Play finished ones too” on a list (`keep_finished`,
  synced with the list, migration 0025) plays them too, from their start (one heard again part way
  resumes), and Play goes round to the top after the last item.
- **Lock screen, car and widget:** while a list plays, the notification's overflow (lock screen,
  watches, Android Auto) has Next in list, which plays the list's next item ahead of Up Next.
  ⏮/⏭ stay the skip by time. Car mode's Next chapter button becomes Next in list in the last
  chapter. The widget and tile keep their play and skip buttons; the list goes on behind them,
  since the service hands over by itself.

The list that plays is kept per profile on the device (`packages/core/src/listPlay.ts`), never
synced: it says what plays now, like the loaded book.

      unfinished item; when it ends the next starts by itself, also with the screen off and the app
      swiped away, and the list screen marks Playing and Next.
- [ ] (adb) While a list plays, the notification's overflow shows Next in list; it starts the
      list's next item (playing if it was playing), and ⏮/⏭ still skip by the skip interval.
- [ ] (adb) A book added with Play next while a list plays comes before the list's next item;
      after it, the list goes on.
- [ ] (adb) The player's ✕ on “From the list …” stops following: the item playing stops at its end
      and Next in list leaves the notification.
- [ ] (adb) A list with “Play finished ones too” and every item finished: Play list starts the
      first item from its start; after the last item, Play list starts at the top again.
- [ ] (adb) A smart list “Not started” plays through in the order it showed, though each item
      leaves the list once it starts.
- [ ] (adb) Car mode in the last chapter of a list's item: the Next chapter button reads Next in
      list and starts the next item.
- [ ] (adb) With a list playing and the app closed, the widget's play and skip buttons work, and
