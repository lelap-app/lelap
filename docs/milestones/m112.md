# M112 Lists 2

[M16](m16.md) made hand-made and smart lists that sync and survive an export. This milestone makes
them something to listen through. Start by listing what exists: how lists are stored and ordered,
and how Up Next works today (casting and the player use it).

- **Play a list:** a Play button on a list starts its first unfinished item and goes on to the next
  one when it ends, books and episodes alike. Smart lists play in their sort order.
- **Where you are in a list:** the list shows what is playing and what comes next; the player says
  which list it is playing from, with a way to stop following it.
- **Finished items:** skip finished ones by default, with a choice to keep them (for a list of
  favourites to play again).
- **Lists on the lock screen, in the car and on the widget:** next and previous move through the
  list where that makes sense, without breaking chapter skipping.
