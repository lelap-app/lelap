# M78 Casting 4

Casting work left out of M69. The rest of its plan moved to [M125](m125.md).

- **Speaker groups (M26):** “Play on several” in the Cast sheet ticks two or more speakers of any
  protocols (a Sonos and a Nest speaker, say), and “Play on N speakers” plays the book on all of
  them. The first one ticked leads: `GroupRenderer` in `modules/cast` (`Group.kt`) sends every
  command to all speakers at once and reports the leader's status, so `RemoteSession`, the
  notification and the player follow it unchanged. `GroupSync` keeps the others in step: one
  more than 1 s off is moved to the leader's place, aiming further ahead by what its last seek
  cost it (up to 4 s), at most every 8 s and not in the 8 s after a command. A follower that
  missed a Play or Pause is told again. Volume keeps each speaker's share. Each follower's calls
  run on a thread of its own, one at a time. A follower that can't be reached, fails a command,
  drops out or gives no status for about 10 s leaves the group, and the rest play on; the sheet
  names one that couldn't be reached. The group plays only at speeds every speaker can.
- **Device authentication, enforced (M26, M69):** `CastRoots` bundles Google's two Cast roots
  (Cast Root CA and Eureka Root CA), byte for byte as Open Screen's `cast/common/certificate` and
  Chromium's older `components/cast_certificate` ship them, each checked against its SHA-256
  fingerprint on load. `CastAuth` now walks the chain itself (name, key, signature, dates, CA
  flags) instead of PKIX, signs over the echoed nonce as Chromium does (old firmware echoes
  none), and reads the audio-only policy into the verdict (logged, not enforced). `CastLink`
  waits for the verdict of every Google Cast speaker it connects to, and the verdict counts only
  if the session's own connection showed the same TLS certificate as the probe's, so nothing on
  the network can pass the probe on to a real device and answer the session itself. The rule:
  only a trusted speaker connects. Any failure (another nonce, a bad signature, a chain that
  doesn't reach Google's roots, a certificate out of its dates, another TLS certificate, no
  answer) ends the attempt, and the sheet says “{name} didn’t prove it’s a genuine Google Cast
  device, so Lelap didn’t connect.” with “Connect anyway”, which lets in that one speaker until
  the app restarts. A real Google speaker
  always answers, so this never locks one out: a fault on our side costs a tap. Google's
  revocation list isn't checked, as Chromium doesn't by default. See
  [Decisions](../brief.md#decisions).
- **Timing lines (M69):** beta 7 logged no receiver timings. `CastLink` now logs every load it
  sends (`cast load-call 840 ms`) itself, so the next round shows whether loads reach the
  receiver at all, apart from `RemoteSession`'s timing lines in `RemotePlayer`.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) [cast] Connect to the Nest Mini and the SHIELD in turn: `adb logcat -s LelapCast`
      shows `cast device auth: trusted` (a speaker may say `trusted, audio only`) and casting
      works as before, with no “Connect anyway” in the sheet. Any `untrusted: …` line from a
      Google device is a fault in `CastAuth`, `another TLS certificate on the session` included
      (a speaker that shows each connection its own certificate): write the reason here.
- [ ] (adb) [cast] Cast a book to the Nest Mini: logcat shows a `cast load-call … ms` line, then
      `cast load … ms`; press Play, Pause and skip back and look for `play`, `pause` and `seek`
      lines. Write which appear; no `load` line after a `load-call` line means `RemoteSession`
      never measures.
- [ ] (you) [cast] With a second phone or laptop on the Wi-Fi running a Cast receiver that isn't
      Google's (for example a desktop receiver app advertising Google Cast), cast to it: the sheet
      says it didn't prove it's a genuine Google Cast device and offers “Connect anyway”; that
      connects and plays. After restarting the app it asks again.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Play on several: tick the Sonos Arc and the Nest Mini, then “Play on 2
      speakers”: both play the book, within about a second of each other after 10 s. The player
      and the notification say “Sonos Arc + Nest Mini” (or the names they have).
- [ ] (you) [cast] In that group, pause, play, skip back 30 s and skip to the next chapter from
      the notification: both speakers follow each time, and within about 10 s they are back in
      step.
- [ ] (you) [cast] In that group, press Volume + and − in the Cast sheet: both speakers get louder
      and quieter, the quieter one staying quieter.
- [ ] (you) [cast] In that group, unplug the Nest Mini: the Sonos plays on alone. Unplug the Sonos
      (the leader) instead: within about 15 s the phone takes over, paused.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Let a group play across a part's end: both speakers move to the next part, and

- [ ] (you) [cast] A group of a Sonos and a UPnP renderer that only plays at 1×, with the book at
      1.5×: the group plays at 1×.

      state, and “Play on 2 speakers” and “Connect anyway” are reachable.
