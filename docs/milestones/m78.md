# M78 Casting 4

Casting work left out of M69.

- Speaker groups across protocols: one book on a Sonos and a Cast speaker at once, kept in step.
  (M26)
- Cast device authentication, enforced: bundle Google's Cast root certificates in
  `CastAuthProbe.roots`, their fingerprints checked against Chromium's
  `components/cast_certificate`, once M69's open check shows what real receivers answer. Then
  decide what a receiver that fails it gets (a warning in the Cast sheet, or no connection), and
  check the device certificate's policies (audio-only devices) and Google's revocation list. (M69)
- An FCast receiver that goes silent: `FCastRenderer` moves its place on from the last update
  without limit until the connection drops, as Google Cast did before M69. How often FCast
  receivers send updates while playing needs a real one first. (M51)
- Tune `RemoteSession`'s waits (2.5 s after a command, 5 s after a load, 10 s before a part's end)
  from the numbers M69's open check writes down. (M50)
