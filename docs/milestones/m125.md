# M125 Casting 5

What [M78](m78.md) left. Tuning the receiver waits needs numbers from real receivers first, so it
moved to [M139](m139.md), with the leading speaker leaving a group.

- **An FCast receiver that goes silent (M51):** `FCastRenderer` pings the receiver every 3 s.
  `FCastWatch` (`modules/cast`, `FCast.kt`) holds a receiver that has answered a ping to the
  same rule as Google Cast: the place stops moving 3 s after its last word, and after 15 s it
  counts as lost and the phone takes over, paused. A late poll (the phone slept) gives it a fresh
  chance. A receiver that never answers a ping keeps the place moving until the connection drops,
  as before, since how often receivers send updates isn't fixed.
- **Bedtime volume while casting (M86):** `VolumeMixer` gives a receiver the sleep timer's fade
  times the bedtime routine's volume (never the call duck). `RemotePlayer` keeps that level and
  applies it once the receiver says its volume, so a routine that started before casting carries
  over; `ReceiverFade` lowers the receiver's own volume and puts it back when the timer ends. A
  group goes through `GroupRenderer.setVolume`, so each speaker keeps its share.
- **“Connect anyway” pinned by certificate (M78):** a Google Cast speaker that fails device
  authentication is refused with the SHA-256 of the TLS certificate its session's connection
  showed. “Connect anyway” pins that fingerprint, not the discovery id, until the app restarts
  (`CastAuthProbe.letIn`, `CastModule`). Another device that takes over the speaker's name shows
  another certificate and is refused again. The device certificate isn't used: a speaker that
  fails often sends none. See [Decisions](../brief.md#decisions).
- **Speaker groups:** a group the listener played on is remembered (the `castGroups` setting, four
  at most, newest first; `packages/core/src/castGroups.ts`) and shows at the top of the Cast sheet
  as one row, “Played together before”, when all its speakers are found; “Forget” drops it. While
  a book plays on a speaker or a group, every other speaker in the sheet has “Add”, and every
  speaker that follows in a group has “Take out”. `CastLink.join` connects the speaker (with
  device authentication, as on connect), loads the file at the group's place on its own thread
  and lets `GroupSync` bring it in step; a single receiver becomes a group it leads.
  `RememberingRenderer` keeps the leader's file and speed for that. A speaker that can't play at
  the book's speed is refused with a message. A follower that drops out disappears from the sheet
  (`members` in the status event).
- **Cast on the web (M26, M67):** the player's Cast tool appears when the browser can reach a
  speaker (Remote Playback API: Chrome's Cast, Safari's AirPlay). The web Cast sheet
  (`CastSheet.web.tsx`) has one button that opens the browser's own list. While a receiver plays,
  `audio.web.ts` keeps the same `<audio>` element for every part and leaves the sound effects and
  skip silence off; a routed element is swapped for a plain one at the same place before the list
  opens, within the tap, and the effects come back when casting ends or is cancelled. Downloaded
  books can't be cast from the browser: their files live in the page's storage, which a receiver
  can't reach. Google's Cast web sender SDK was not used: the web app's Content-Security-Policy
  allows scripts from its own origin only (`script-src 'self'`), and the SDK loads Google's
  scripts and frames. The Remote Playback API needs no script and no change to the policy.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) [cast] Cast a book to an FCast receiver (the FCast receiver app on a TV or laptop),
      then pull its network cable or turn off its Wi-Fi while it plays: within about 20 s the phone
      takes over, paused, no more than a few seconds past where the receiver stopped.
- [ ] (adb) [cast] Cast to an FCast receiver and leave it paused for 10 minutes: the session stays,
      and `adb logcat -s LelapCast` shows no lost connection.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Turn on the bedtime routine with the volume at 50 % and a window that is open
      now, play a book on the phone, then cast it to the Nest Mini: the Nest plays at about half
      the volume it had. When the timer ends it pauses, and its volume is back where it was.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Cast to the Sonos Arc and start a 10-minute timer by hand: in the last minute
      the Sonos fades, pauses at the end and gets its volume back.
- [ ] (you) [cast] In a group of the Sonos Arc and the Nest Mini with the bedtime routine at 50 %:
      both get quieter, the quieter one staying quieter, and both get their volume back after.
- [ ] (you) [cast] With a non-Google Cast receiver on the Wi-Fi (a desktop receiver app), cast to
      it and choose “Connect anyway”: it connects and plays. Cast to it again: it connects without
      asking. Restart the receiver app so it makes a new certificate (or run a second one under
      the same name), cast again: the sheet asks again.
- [ ] (adb) [cast] Connect to the Nest Mini and the SHIELD: casting works as before, with no
      “Connect anyway” and `cast device auth: trusted` in `adb logcat -s LelapCast`.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Play on the Sonos Arc and the Nest Mini with “Play on several”, then hand back
      to the phone and open the Cast sheet again: “Sonos Arc + Nest Mini, Played together before”
      is at the top; one tap plays on both. “Forget” removes the row.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Cast to the Sonos Arc, then press “Add” on the Nest Mini: within a few seconds
      the Nest plays the same place, and within about 10 s both are in step. The player says
      “Sonos Arc + Nest Mini”. Press “Take out” on the Nest: it stops and the Sonos plays on.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Cast at 1.5× to the Sonos Arc and press “Add” on a UPnP renderer that only plays
      at 1×: the sheet says it only plays at 1×, and the Sonos plays on.
- [ ] (you) [cast] In a group, unplug the follower: within about 15 s it disappears from the
      player's name and the sheet no longer shows “Take out” for it.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Web app in Chrome on a laptop on the same Wi-Fi as the Nest Mini, a streamed
      book playing with voice boost on: the player shows the Cast tool; “Choose a speaker or TV”
      opens Chrome's list; picking the Nest plays the book there from the same place. Skip to the
      next chapter in another part: it goes on on the Nest. Stop casting from Chrome's list: the
      book goes on in the browser, with voice boost.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Web app in Safari on a Mac or iPhone with an AirPlay speaker: the same as above
      through AirPlay.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Web app in Chrome with a downloaded book: casting it says it can't be cast from
      the browser, or the Cast tool doesn't show, and the book plays on in the browser.
- [ ] (you) Malay: a native speaker checks the 17 new lines in `ms.json` (Played together before,
      Take out, Choose a speaker or TV, the web casting text and the rest).
