# M125 Casting 5

What [M78](m78.md) left.

- An FCast receiver that goes silent: `FCastRenderer` moves its place on from the last update
  without limit until the connection drops, as Google Cast did before M69. How often FCast
  receivers send updates while playing needs a real one first. (M51)
- Tune `RemoteSession`'s waits (2.5 s after a command, 5 s after a load, 10 s before a part's end)
  from the numbers M69's and M78's open checks write down. (M50)
- A Cast tool in the web app, through the Remote Playback API (Chrome's Cast, Safari's AirPlay).
  The web player swaps its `<audio>` element when the sound effects turn on or off, which would
  drop a remote session, and the Cast sheet's device list has no browser picker to stand in for.
  (M26, M67)
- The bedtime routine's quieter volume while casting: `RemotePlayer` takes the sleep timer's fade
  but not `VolumeMixer.bedtime`, so a receiver keeps its own volume. Scale its fade by the
  routine's gain, and restore it when the timer ends. With a group, scale each speaker's share.
  (M86)
- “Connect anyway” lets a speaker in by its discovery id, which another device on the Wi-Fi can
  take over until the app restarts. Pin what the listener accepted instead: the device
  certificate's or TLS certificate's fingerprint. (M78)
- Speaker groups: remember a group the listener played on, so it is one tap the next time, and
  let a speaker join or leave a group that plays.
