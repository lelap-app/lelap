# M31 Discover 2

Audiobook covers in Home rows, the Discover grid and Search carry a badge: “On this phone” for a
library-folder book of the same title and author or a finished download, the add-on that streams
it, or “Metadata only” once every add-on that could have a stream answered without one. Podcasts
get none. The answers come from `packages/core/src/addons/availability.ts`, which asks only for
visible items, two at a time, and caches them per work (found 24 h, none 6 h); stream requests go
to the add-ons, never through the proxy.

Discover has a “Playable only” toggle that hides items known to be “Metadata only”. On Android the
background refresh (M11) checks up to ten works from the profile's want-to-read shelves and posts
“Now playable: <title>” when one gains a source; the notification permission is asked once per
profile from a card under the Want to read row, never at launch.
