# M31 Discover 2

**Status:** in progress (PR open). "Playable only" filter, source badges on covers, alerts when a wanted book gets a source.

- **Badges** on audiobook covers in Home rows, the Discover grid and Search results: “On this
  phone” (amber fill) for a library-folder book with the same title and author or a finished
  download, the add-on that streams it (“LibriVox”, a server's add-on name; amber outline), or
  “Metadata only” (muted outline) once every add-on that could have a stream answered without
  one. Nothing shows while the answer is out or when an add-on did not answer. Podcasts get no
  badge; they play from their feeds.
- **How it stays cheap** (`packages/core/src/addons/availability.ts`): local matches come from one
  read of the library per change. Streams go through M15's `findSources`; when no add-on has the
  work by the catalog's ID, its meta is read once for external IDs (a `hardcover:` book is
  `ol:` to LibriVox). `SourceCache` remembers answers per work (found: 24 h, none: 6 h, unknown:
  not kept), asks only for items the lists report as visible, newest first, two at a time and
  250 ms apart, and keeps at most 40 waiting (older ones scrolled away). The item page feeds what
  it found into the cache. The cache starts over when the profile's add-ons change. Stream
  requests go to the add-ons, never through the proxy.
- **Discover:** a “Playable only” toggle hides items known to be “Metadata only” and says how many
  are hidden; items still unknown stay.
- **Wanted books:** the background refresh (M11, Android) reads every `want-to-read` shelf,
  remembers its works per profile (settings rows `wanted:<type>:<id>`, no migration, never
  synced), drops works gone from every shelf (only when all shelves were read), and asks again
  for up to ten works without a source. A work that gets one posts “Now playable: <title>”;
  a tap opens its item page. The first check is a baseline, so a book that played all along
  never alerts. Notifications come from the download module (`notify`, channel “Wanted books”),
  without Google Play services.
- **Permission:** asked once per profile, from a card under the Want to read row on Home (Android
  13+, only when not yet allowed), never at launch. Downloads still ask on the first download.
- Device checks: [`m31-follow-ups.md`](../m31-follow-ups.md).
