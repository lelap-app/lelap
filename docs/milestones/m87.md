# M87 Stats and year in sound

The Stats screen (`apps/app/app/stats.tsx`) builds on M51's listening log and shows, per profile:

- **Time:** today, this week, this year and in all; bars for the last 7 days and the last 8 weeks,
  with each chart's total. Weeks start on the region's first day (`firstDayOfWeek` in
  `packages/core/src/locale.ts`, with a CLDR table where Hermes has no week info).
- **Streaks:** the current one and the longest, with its dates.
- **Finished books:** this year's count against the goal, and their titles with the day each was
  finished. Podcast episodes don't count as books.
- **Year in sound:** one row per year with listening. The overview (`apps/app/app/year.tsx`) has
  time listened, days listened, books finished, books and episodes played, the longest streak, the
  busiest day, a bar per month, the finished books and the most listened.
- **Share as image:** the year as a 1080 × 1350 PNG, drawn on the phone with react-native-svg's
  `toDataURL` and handed to the share sheet (on the web, a download). Only counts and the most
  listened title are on it. Nothing else leaves the device.

No percentages: bars and counts. Every chart has a text TalkBack reads, each bar with its day,
week or month and its time. Both screens and the image follow the phone's light or dark setting
with car mode's colours. Dates and month names follow the profile's language and the phone's
region through `apps/app/src/format.ts`.

The weeks, streaks and year totals are pure functions in `packages/core/src/stats.ts`, tested in
`stats.test.ts`.
