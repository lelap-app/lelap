# M7 Sync

A pairing code, an encrypted oplog and the “continue on another device” prompt, in
`packages/core` and the sync Worker in `services/sync` (D1 databases `lelap-sync` and
`lelap-sync-staging`; production on `sync.lelap.app`). A sync group is a household: its
encrypted data carries the profiles, and each device remembers its active profile. Design notes:
[the brief](../brief.md#sync). The newest change to a row wins by each device's clock, so a phone
with a wrong clock can win or lose wrongly.

Done when you switch between phone and laptop in the middle of a chapter.

## Setup

D1 `lelap-sync` and `lelap-sync-staging` (their IDs are in `services/sync/wrangler.toml`). Staging
gets its host from `SYNC_STAGING_DOMAIN` in the `web` environment; production's comes with the
release workflow (`SYNC_DOMAIN`, [M8](m8.md#setup)).
