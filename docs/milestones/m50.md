# M50 Casting 2

The playback service's media session plays on the receiver: `CastLink` in `modules/cast` holds
the session, and the service swaps its player for `RemotePlayer`, so the lock screen,
notification, headphones, Android Auto and volume keys control the receiver under the
`mediaPlayback` foreground service. Parts advance natively, the book's end is logged and Up Next
follows on the same receiver (`RemoteSession`), the sleep timer fades the receiver's own volume
and pauses it (`ReceiverFade`), UPnP plays at the speeds the renderer lists, and the Android 17
local network permission is asked from the Cast sheet on every Android 17 device: a Pixel enforced
it at target 36 too, against Android's docs. Casting
decisions are in the [brief](../brief.md#decisions).

Done when a book casts to a speaker for an hour with the phone locked, stops on the sleep timer,
and the next book in Up Next starts on the speaker when one ends.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) Cast a multi-part book to a Sonos and lock the phone for an hour: parts advance, the
      notification shows the chapter and controls the speaker.
- [ ] (you) While casting, lock screen, notification (pause, play, ±30 s and the seek bar), headset
      button, volume keys and Android Auto control the receiver; the phone stays silent. In the car,
      picking another book plays it on the receiver.
- [ ] (you) A 5-minute sleep timer while casting fades the speaker over the last minute (Sonos, Nest
      Mini), pauses it and puts its volume back; on a DLNA TV without RenderingControl it pauses
      without a fade. An end-of-chapter timer pauses it within about two seconds of the chapter's end.
- [ ] (you) A book that ends on a speaker shows as finished in the library; a podcast episode with the
      next episode in Up Next starts that episode on the same speaker.
- [ ] (you) A UPnP renderer that lists 3/2 in its AVTransport description plays at 1.5×; Sonos plays at
      1× and the player shows 1×.
- [ ] (you) Nest Mini: 1.5× still applies; the speaker taken by another phone hands back, paused.
- [ ] (you) Hand back (“Play on this phone”) while playing continues on the phone from the receiver's

      the notification stays and still controls the receiver; reopening the app shows the receiver in
      the Cast tool.
- [ ] (you) On Android 17, before the permission was given: the Cast sheet explains, then asks for

- [ ] (you) The home screen widget and Quick Settings tile follow the receiver's play state while
