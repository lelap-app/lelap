# M139 Casting 6

What [M125](m125.md) left, because it needs numbers from real receivers or a choice first.

- Tune `RemoteSession`'s waits (2.5 s after a command, 5 s after a load, 10 s before a part's
  end) and `FCastWatch`'s (a ping every 3 s, lost after 15 s) from the numbers that M69's, M78's
  and M125's open checks write down. Beta 7 logged no timing lines at all, and M78 added the
  `load-call` line to find out why; until a test round brings numbers, any new value would be a
  guess. (M50, M69)
- The leading speaker of a group can't leave it while it plays: `RemoteSession` follows the
  leader's status, and handing that role to another speaker mid-play needs the follower's calls
  moved onto the cast thread without reordering them. For now the listener starts a new group.
  Decide whether a leader that leaves hands over to the next speaker or ends the group, then build
  it in `GroupRenderer`.
