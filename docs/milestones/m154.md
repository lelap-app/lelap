# M154 Live streams

Asked for by a third-party add-on author on 2026-11-20. `live` is part of protocol revision 14
with [M152](m152.md); an older app plays such a stream as an ordinary episode. Decision in the
brief under “Live streams (M154)”.

- **A live book keeps nothing.** `addStreamedBook` keeps `live` and the stream's `mime` on the
  book's `addonItem` (no migration) and never allows a download. `isLiveBook` in
  `packages/core/src/live.ts` guards `saveProgress` (so Continue listening never lists it),
  `recordListening`, `recordHistory`, `setFinished`, `finishFromPlayer`, `finishAndNext`,
  `downloadAllowed` and `enqueueScrobble`, which lets only `start` through.
- **Android** (`modules/audio`): Media3's HLS module; `LiveStream.kt` turns ICY `StreamTitle` and
  ID3 `TIT2`/`TPE1` into the line under the station's name and gives an HLS address without
  `.m3u8` its MIME type. For a live item the service starts at the default position, drops the
  seek and speed commands and the skip buttons, skips smart rewind and the end-of-book log, goes
  back to what is on air on Play after a pause, and shows the song over “Live” and the station on
  the notification, lock screen, widget and car. `PlayerState` gains `live` and `nowPlaying`.
- **Web** (`apps/app/src/platform/webhls.ts`): the browser's own HLS, else hls.js's light build,
  loaded only then; ID3 cues from the element's metadata text tracks. Skip silence and loudness
  reading stay off for a live stream, and Play after a pause reloads it.
- **Player UI:** “Live” instead of the bar, Play and Pause only, the song under the name, Sound,
  Sleep (minutes) and Cast in the tool row; the mini player shows “Live · song”; no download on
  the item page and no “Mark as finished”.
- **Casting:** a Cast receiver gets `streamType: LIVE` and HLS as `application/x-mpegurl`, without
  the phone's sound effects; UPnP and FCast have no word for live.
- Left out, as planned: station schedules and a `station` content type.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) Play an add-on episode whose stream is a live Icecast MP3 with `live: true` (a public
      broadcaster's station): it starts at what is on air, the player shows “Live” instead of the
      bar, no skip, chapter, speed or bookmark buttons, and the song from ICY under the name.
- [ ] (adb) Play a live HLS stream (`.m3u8`, and one without `.m3u8` but with its `mime`): it plays
      through Media3's HLS module, and timed ID3 titles show under the station's name.
- [ ] (adb) Pause a live stream for a minute, then press Play: it goes on with what is on air now,
      not where it stopped.
- [ ] (adb) After listening to a live stream, Home's Continue listening, the stats and the item's
      history don't list it, its item page offers no download, and a scrobbler add-on gets one
      `start` event only.
- [ ] (adb) The notification, lock screen and widget show the song over “Live” and the station
      name, with no time or seek bar, and the notification has no back or forward buttons.
- [ ] (adb) A normal audiobook and a podcast episode still seek, change speed, rewind after a pause
      and save their place as before.
- [ ] (you) [car] Android Auto shows a live stream's song and “Live”, with Play and Pause only.
- [ ] (you) [cast] Casting a live HLS stream to a Chromecast plays it as live on the TV.
- [ ] (you) [browsers] On the web, a live HLS stream plays in desktop Chrome or Firefox (hls.js)
      and in Safari (its own HLS), with ID3 titles under the name; a live MP3 stream plays without
      a song line.
- [ ] (you) An older app (before revision 14) plays a live stream as an ordinary episode.
