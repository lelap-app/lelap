# M136 Lecture transcripts

What [M132](m132.md) left out: Open lectures shipped audio only, because the protocol had no
transcript for a book part. Yale's lecture pages carry each lecture's transcript under the same
licence, and the [Open lectures](../brief.md#decisions) decision lets the catalog service fetch it.

- **Protocol, revision 12:** `transcripts` on a stream part (`PartTranscript` in
  `packages/sdk/src/protocol.ts`), shaped like a feed's `podcast:transcript`: address, MIME type,
  language. A list that doesn't fit is dropped, not the part, and an older app drops the field and
  still plays. Documented in [the brief](../brief.md#add-on-protocol), the protocol page and the
  SDK's README.
- **The app** keeps each part's transcripts on the book's add-on reference (`AddonItemRef` in
  `packages/core/src/schema.ts`, no migration), read back by `partTranscripts` in
  `packages/core/src/addons/streamed.ts`. The player's Transcript button and the transcript screen
  (`apps/app/app/transcript.tsx`, [M23](m23.md)'s, for episodes) follow the playing part: the
  lecture's title on top, the course under it, and the next lecture brings its own text.
- **The service** answers `/lectures/transcript/{course}/{n}.txt`
  (`services/catalog/src/lectures/transcript.ts`): the `inline_content` block of Yale's lecture
  page as paragraphs, Yale's credit line first, UTF-8 plain text with CORS, cached for a month.
  Only text goes through it; the audio still streams from Yale or archive.org. Every Open lectures
  stream lists one transcript per lecture, on the request's own origin or `CATALOG_ORIGIN`. A
  recorded part of Lecture 1 of Roman Architecture
  (`services/catalog/test/fixtures/yale-hsar-252-lecture-1-transcript.html`, kept as Yale serves
  it, so Prettier skips it) tests the extraction.
