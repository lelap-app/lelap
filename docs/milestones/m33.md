# M33 Everyday Android

Wear OS controls, car mode, a bedtime sleep timer and a low headphone battery banner, with nothing
that needs Google Play services, so the F-Droid build gets all of it (see
[Decisions](../brief.md#decisions)).

- **Wear OS:** no watch app. The media session offers back and forward by the skip interval (the
  seconds on the icon) and the sleep timer button as media button preferences, which watches, the
  lock screen and cars show (`modules/audio`; what a watch shows is in
  [`modules/audio/README.md`](../../modules/audio/README.md#wear-os)).
- **Car mode** (`apps/app/app/car.tsx`): a full-screen player with five big buttons on black, the
  screen kept on, opened from the car button in the player. With “Offer car mode in the car” on,
  Lelap offers it when the phone connects to a car's Bluetooth (device class car audio or
  hands-free).
- **Bedtime:** a window and a length or the end of the chapter in Settings → Playback; when Play
  falls inside the window the player service starts the timer itself, once per night, also with
  the app closed (`Bedtime.kt`).
- **Low headphone battery:** an opt-in banner when the connected headphones likely won't last the
  sleep timer or the rest of the chapter (at least 20 % per hour assumed, a quarter more wanted).
  The level comes from Android's battery broadcast through the new `modules/bluetooth`; the
  nearby devices permission is asked when a setting that needs it is turned on.
