# M33 Everyday Android

**Status:** planned. Wear OS controls, car mode, bedtime sleep timer; nothing that needs Google Play services. Details: [`next.md`](../next.md).

**Idea (2026-11-17): low headphone battery.** A banner like the offline one when the connected
Bluetooth headphones or speaker report a low battery (“Headphones at 35%”). Android reports many
devices' level through a system broadcast; it needs the nearby-devices permission
(`BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`), so it is an opt-in setting. Devices that don't report a level show nothing,
and the web can't read it. Rather than a fixed 50%, warn when the battery likely won't last what is
coming: the sleep timer or the rest of the chapter.
