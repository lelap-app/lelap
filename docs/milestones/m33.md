# M33 Everyday Android

**Status:** in progress (from [`next.md`](../next.md)). Wear OS controls, car mode, a bedtime sleep
timer and a low headphone battery banner; nothing that needs Google Play services, so the F-Droid
build gets all of it. Phone checks are in [`m33-follow-ups.md`](../m33-follow-ups.md).

- **Wear OS:** no watch app. The media session offers back and forward by the skip interval (with
  the seconds on the icon) and the sleep timer button as media button preferences, which watches,
  the lock screen and cars show. What a watch shows is in
  [`modules/audio/README.md`](../../modules/audio/README.md#wear-os).
- **Car mode:** a full-screen player with five big buttons (back, play or pause, forward, next
  chapter, sleep timer) on black, with the screen kept on. It opens from the car button in the
  player. With “Offer car mode in the car” on, Lelap asks to open it when the phone connects to a
  car's Bluetooth (device class car audio or hands-free); that needs the nearby devices permission,
  asked for when the setting is turned on.
- **Bedtime:** “Start a sleep timer at bedtime” in Settings → Playback: a window (half-hour steps,
  may cross midnight) and a length or the end of the chapter. When Play falls inside the window,
  the player service starts the timer by itself, once per night, also with the app closed
  (`Bedtime.kt`, tested on the JVM).
- **Low headphone battery (idea of 2026-11-17):** an opt-in setting. A banner like the offline one
  in the Library, the player and car mode when the connected headphones or speaker likely won't
  last the sleep timer, or else the rest of the chapter. The estimate assumes at least 20 % per
  hour and wants a quarter more than what is coming; with nothing to measure against it warns at
  20 %. Devices that report no level show nothing; the web shows nothing. The level comes from
  Android's battery broadcast, read by the new `modules/bluetooth`, with the nearby devices
  permission asked for when the setting is turned on.

**Done when** the checks in the follow-ups pass on a phone, a Wear OS watch and in a car.
