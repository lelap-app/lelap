# M72 Languages 2

Text that still showed in English, or in the phone’s language instead of the profile’s.

- **Dates and numbers** go through `Intl` with the profile’s language tag (`localeTag` in
  `packages/core/src/locale.ts`, used by `apps/app/src/format.ts`) and the phone’s region,
  whatever the phone’s language: English on a Danish phone is `en-DK` and writes 18/11/2026, not
  the US order, while month and weekday names follow the profile. Episode dates,
  the last sync time, the update date, the weekdays in Listening, sizes and a book’s language name
  follow it; durations (“3 h 12 min left”) go through `t()`.
- **Native errors** go through `NativeText` like the rest of Kotlin’s lines: failed downloads
  (`Http.kt`, `DownloadWorker.kt`), casting (`modules/cast` now depends on `modules/text`;
  `NativeText.current()` serves the receivers’ threads), app updates (`modules/updater`, the same),
  and “Casting stopped”. A Bluetooth device without a name comes back empty and the app names it.
- **Made-up chapter titles**: ID3 chapters without a title come back empty from Kotlin and are
  named `t('Chapter {n}')` when the scan saves them; podcast chapters files take the same name
  through an `untitled` parameter. Jellyfin and Audiobookshelf keep “Chapter 3”: they are
  rescanned in full each time, and a name that changes with the profile would rewrite their
  chapters on every scan (see [M99](m99.md)).
- **Translation check** finds a key written twice in a catalog, and a plural pair whose first
  `t()` has calls in its values.
- **Untranslated text**: the headphone battery banner, the car mode offer, the item page’s
  no-audio card, download states, the storage line on Downloads, the transcript error and the
  “Now playable” notification.

Media3’s own play and pause descriptions still follow the phone’s language; that and the rest
left out are in [M99](m99.md).
