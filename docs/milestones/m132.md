# M132 Open lectures

Free university lecture courses as books: one course is one book, one lecture one part. It starts
with Open Yale Courses, from a hand-kept list of complete courses. One of Lelap's own add-ons, off by
default: [Lelap's own add-ons](../brief.md#decisions) and [Open lectures](../brief.md#decisions) in
the brief.

## What shipped

- **The add-on** runs on the catalog service: `services/catalog/src/lectures/` answers
  `/lectures/manifest.json` as `app.lelap.lectures`. One catalog, “Open Yale Courses”, with
  `genre` (Yale's departments), `search` (title, lecturer, code, department) and `skip`, so it
  shows on Home and in Search. A course is an `audiobook` with IDs `oyc:{code}`, such as
  `oyc:hsar-252`; the lecturer is its author, the department its genre, Yale's course page its
  `link`. It asks nobody at runtime: the list is in the code.
- **Audio never touches our servers.** The stream is one multi-part stream of addresses on
  `oyc.yale.edu` or `archive.org`; `course.ts` refuses any other host. One stream per course, so
  places stay keyed to one `sourceKey`.
- **Credit and licence on every course.** Each description ends with Yale's credit line in the
  form its terms set out (“Diana E. E. Kleiner, Roman Architecture (Yale University: Open Yale
  Courses), http://oyc.yale.edu. License: Creative Commons BY-NC-SA”), then the licence's name and
  address and Yale's terms of use.
- **No video.** Yale's YouTube embeds and archive.org's five video items are never used; a lecture
  that exists only as video keeps its course off the list.
- **Offered, off by default.** `hostedAddons` in `apps/app/src/config.ts` now says how each of our
  hosted add-ons is set up: `configure` (Hardcover, gPodder: Set up opens their page) or `add`
  (Open lectures: Add installs the manifest after the usual review sheet). `OwnAddons.tsx` draws
  the offer under Settings → Library and sources → Add-ons and in the first-start welcome. Lepak ([M131](m131.md)) can
  use `add` too.
- **The list** is `services/catalog/src/lectures/courses.ts`, written by
  `scripts/open-lectures.mts` and read by hand. Run it again to refresh the list; it keeps Yale's
  and archive.org's answers in a cache folder and prints, per course, the host and why a course is
  left out.
- **Transcripts are not in this milestone.** The protocol has no transcript for a book part, and
  adding a revision was out of scope here; [M136](m136.md) plans it.
- **Tests** (`services/catalog/test/lectures.test.ts`): every course against the schema (every
  lecture has audio from one host and a length), no video, Roman Architecture against recorded
  Yale pages and archive.org's file list, the credit line on every course, catalog, search, genre
  and paging. No test reaches the network.

## Audio hosts, sampled 2026-11-19

Yale's course list has 39 courses. Its sessions tables leave lectures out (Roman Architecture
lists 16 of 24), so the script walks each lecture page's Previous and Next links instead. Sessions
with neither audio nor video (exams) are not lectures. Every Yale MP3 was asked with `HEAD`;
archive.org's copies were matched by file size, which also gave their lengths.

- **From Yale (34):** AFAM 162, AMST 246, ASTR 160, BENG 100, CHEM 125a, CHEM 125b, CLCV 205,
  EEB 122, ECON 252 (2008), ECON 252 (2011), ECON 251, ECON 159, ENGL 300, ENGL 220, ENGL 310,
  ENGL 291, EVST 255, HIST 116, HIST 119, HIST 210, HIST 202, HIST 234, HIST 251, HIST 276,
  HSAR 252, ITAL 310, PHIL 181, PHYS 201, PLSC 114, PSYC 110, RLST 152, RLST 145, SOCY 151,
  SPAN 300. Every lecture's MP3 answered `200 audio/mpeg`.
- **From archive.org (3):** MCDB 150 (Yale's lecture 24 answers 503), PHIL 176 (lecture 4 answers
  404), PHYS 200 (lecture 4 answers 503). Their copies match Yale's lectures in count and title.
- **Left out (3):** GG 140 (a field trip is video only), PLSC 270 (a guest lecture has no audio),
  PSYC 123 (a guest lecture has no audio at Yale; archive.org has one, but a lecture Yale holds
  back may be held back on purpose).
- **Order:** Yale's, which is the order the lectures were given. Roman Architecture's Paper
  Topics session comes after lecture 13, where archive.org numbers it 24.
- **Lengths:** from archive.org's file list where the copy is the same file; otherwise (6
  lectures in ASTR 160, HIST 116 and PHYS 201) from the MP3's bit rate and size.
- **Credits:** no lecture page has a Credits section in its text. Each links a PDF that lists
  the images shown in the lecture, which audio doesn't carry.
- **Web app:** Yale's MP3s play in the browser, and its server answers range requests, but it
  sends no CORS headers: the web app can't download them or run them through the sound effects.
  archive.org's can.
