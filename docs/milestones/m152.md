# M152 Add-on protocol 5

Protocol revision 14, backward compatible like every revision. Asked for by a third-party add-on
author on 2026-11-20; each item is generic, and the repo's docs and tests use open sources for
their examples. Builds on [M144](m144.md)'s published SDK.

- **Streams that are still preparing.** A stream URL that answers `503` with `Retry-After`
  (seconds or an HTTP date, held between 5 seconds and an hour) makes the player show
  “Preparing…” and try again by itself when it says. An optional `availability` (`ready`, the
  default, or `preparing`) says so up front, so the item page can mark such streams without a
  request. A preparing stream keeps its `sourceKey` when it becomes ready, and no place is saved
  while it prepares. Examples in the docs: an Internet Archive item whose files are still being
  derived, a server whose disks are waking up.
  - Preparing can take minutes or hours. The player keeps trying for 10 minutes while it is open;
    the listener can cancel, or choose **Download when ready**, which hands the stream to the
    download queue. The queue treats `503` with `Retry-After` the same way, without a limit of
    its own, keeps the Wi-Fi and charging rules, and the download's usual notification says when
    the book is ready.
  - The add-on ends the waiting with any other answer. `404` or `410` means it won't come: the
    player and the queue stop, and show the answer's body when it is plain text of at most 200
    characters (“The source no longer has this file.”), else a message of the app's own.
- **Parts without lengths.** `durationMs` becomes optional on a `MultiPartStream`'s parts. The app
  learns each part's length when it opens it (Media3 on Android, the media element on the web),
  keeps it with the book, and shows the total once every part is known. Until then the book's
  progress counts parts, and seeking past an unmeasured part opens it first. The app never sends
  measured lengths back to the add-on.
- **A second look for slow answers.** An optional `refreshAfter` (seconds) on a stream response
  says the answer is incomplete: while the item page is open, the app asks once more after that
  delay and adds new streams below the ones shown, without reordering the list. That request
  skips the app's own cache, and its answer replaces the cached one.
- **Stream fields:** `format` (such as `m4b`, `mp3`, `flac`; `mime` can't tell M4B from M4A),
  `language` (BCP 47, as on metas and editions) and `releaseId`, the same for the same files
  across add-ons. `releaseId` is a URN in lowercase (`urn:<namespace>:<id>`, at most 200
  characters), compared as a whole string, so add-ons that share a source agree by using the URN
  form that source already has. A source with no shared ID leaves it out; `lelap-sdk check` warns
  of one that isn't a lowercase URN. [M153](m153.md) uses them.
- **Metadata chapters for multi-part streams.** The meta's chapters are used for a multi-part
  stream without chapters of its own when the parts' total matches the meta's `durationMs`
  within 15 seconds, as a single file's length already does.
- `lelap-sdk check` warns of `availability` or `refreshAfter` without revision 14, of a
  `refreshAfter` above 30 seconds, and of an unknown `format`.

**Done when** a test add-on that answers `503` with `Retry-After: 5` plays on its own after the
wait, a book of eight unmeasured parts shows its total after each part was opened once, and an
older app still plays every stream a revision-14 add-on sends.
