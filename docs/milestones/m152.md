# M152 Add-on protocol 5

Protocol revision 14, asked for by a third-party add-on author on 2026-11-20 and backward
compatible: an older app ignores the new fields, plays a preparing stream as if it were ready and
skips a stream with a part that has no length. The decision is in the brief under
[Preparing streams, unmeasured parts, a second look and stream identity](../brief.md#add-on-protocol).

## What shipped

- **SDK** (`packages/sdk/src/protocol.ts`, `LATEST_PROTOCOL_VERSION` 14): a stream's
  `availability`, `format` (`STREAM_FORMATS`), `language` and `releaseId` (`RELEASE_ID`, a
  lowercase URN); `durationMs` optional on a `StreamPart`; `refreshAfter` on stream responses.
  `lelap-sdk check` warns of `availability` or `refreshAfter` without revision 14, a
  `refreshAfter` above 30 seconds, an unknown `format`, a `releaseId` that isn't a lowercase URN
  and parts without lengths, and takes audio answering `503` with `Retry-After` for preparing.
  Tests in `revision14.test.ts`; a changeset waits for the next release.
- **Preparing streams.** `packages/core/src/addons/preparing.ts` reads `Retry-After` (seconds or
  an HTTP date, 5 s to an hour), the add-on's short plain-text reason for `404` and `410`, and
  probes a stream's first byte. For a stream marked `availability: preparing` the item page
  (`apps/app/src/components/Preparing.tsx`) probes before Play, never for a downloaded copy, shows
  “Preparing…” with a countdown, asks again when told for up to 10 minutes, and offers Cancel and
  Download when ready; nothing plays, so no place is saved, meanwhile. Only marked streams are
  probed: a server that ignores `Range` would send the whole file, which the phone's `fetch`
  reads in full. Source rows say “Preparing”.
- **Download when ready.** `DownloadRules.decide` answers `PREPARING` for `503` with
  `Retry-After`; `Http.fetch` waits inside the worker's run with no limit of its own, so the Wi-Fi
  and charging rules still stop it (Android 15 also stops a data-sync foreground run after six
  hours; WorkManager starts it again), and shows “Preparing… downloads when ready” on the download's
  notification. A download that waited posts “Downloaded and ready to play.”. `404` and `410`
  show the add-on's words. The web app's downloads wait the same way while the page is open.
- **Parts without lengths.** `knownTotal` keeps a streamed book's total at 0 until every part has
  a length, in `addStreamedBook` and `learnFileDuration` (a local book keeps its scan's sum); `LibraryBook.partsDone` and `bookFraction`
  (`packages/core/src/library/queries.ts`) count progress by parts until then, on library rows,
  cover badges and the item page. No per-part chapters while a part is unmeasured.
- **A second look.** `fetchStreamAnswer` reads `refreshAfter`; `findSources` lists the add-ons that
  asked, and `refreshSources` asks them once more with `cache: 'no-store'`, adding new streams
  below (`mergeSources`). The item page schedules it once and replaces the cached answer
  (`refreshItemSources` in `apps/app/src/addons.ts`); a stream shown already takes the newer
  answer in its place, so one that was preparing shows as ready.
- **Metadata chapters for parts** already applied when the parts' total fits the meta's length
  within 15 seconds (`metaChapters` in `packages/core/src/addons/streamed.ts`); unmeasured parts
  never fit, so a book gets them once its parts are measured and it is added again.
- The protocol page documents it all; the guide's source step sends `format`.

Left for [M155](m155.md): audio that answers `503` with `Retry-After` without being marked
`preparing`, and a preparing part in the middle of a book, which only the player meets;
seeking past an unmeasured part by opening it first; the meta's chapters as soon as the last part
is measured.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) A test add-on with a stream marked `availability: preparing` whose URL answers `503`
      with `Retry-After: 5` twice, then the audio: the source row says “Preparing”, Play shows
      “Preparing…” with a countdown, then plays on its own.
- [ ] (adb) The same add-on, Download when ready: the download notification says “Preparing…
      downloads when ready”, the download finishes after the wait, and “Downloaded and ready to
      play.” follows. With “Wi-Fi only” on and Wi-Fi off, it waits for Wi-Fi.
- [ ] (adb) A stream URL that answers `410` with `text/plain` “The source no longer has this
      file.” while preparing: the item page's alert and the failed download show those words.
- [ ] (adb) A book of eight parts without `durationMs`: the library row shows a percentage by
      parts while playing, and the total appears once each part was opened once.
- [ ] (adb) An add-on answering `refreshAfter: 5` with one stream, then two: the second appears
      below the first on the open item page after about five seconds, the first stays selected.
- [ ] (you) The web app with a preparing stream on a server that sends CORS and
      `Access-Control-Expose-Headers: Retry-After`: Play shows “Preparing…” and plays after it.
- [ ] (you) An app from before revision 14 with a revision-14 add-on: every stream with lengths
      plays; a stream of unmeasured parts is skipped, nothing crashes.
