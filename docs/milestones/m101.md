# M101 Better sound 2

What [M84](m84.md) left out.

- The web player has voice boost only. Even loudness could measure the level with an
  `AnalyserNode` and read ReplayGain tags from the first bytes of a file; skip silence needs the
  audio decoded ahead, or an `AudioWorklet` that drops quiet frames and keeps the place right.
- On Android, Media3's silence processor runs after Lelap's own: voice boost and even loudness
  can lift room noise over its -30 dBFS threshold, so fewer pauses count as silence. Running a
  second silence processor in front of `SoundProcessor`, switched by the service, would fix it.
- Receivers that could apply the effects themselves get none; no protocol Lelap casts with
  offers them today.
