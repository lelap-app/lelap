# M101 Better sound 2

What [M84](m84.md) left out.

- The web player has voice boost only. Even loudness could measure the level with an
  `AnalyserNode` and read ReplayGain tags from the first bytes of a file; skip silence needs the
  audio decoded ahead, or an `AudioWorklet` that drops quiet frames and keeps the place right.
- Time saved is banked to the book when the service switches books, before the player's flush
  counts the old book's last few hundred milliseconds; those go to the next book. Counting by
  media item in the audio thread would fix it.
- ReplayGain changes when the player moves to the next part, a moment after the audio thread
  has started it, so a part's first moment can play at the previous part's gain.
- Receivers that could apply the effects themselves get none; no protocol Lelap casts with
  offers them today.
