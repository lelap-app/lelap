# M11 Background engine

Up Next hands over in the playback service (`modules/audio`) with the app closed, and logs what
finished for the app to fold in. Feeds, auto-downloads and sync run in the background about once
an hour through `expo-background-task` (WorkManager underneath).

Done when a queued episode starts after the previous one with the phone locked and the app swiped
away, and a new episode of an auto-download podcast is on the phone the next morning.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) A book in Up Next stops at its end; the episode queued after it does not start.

- [ ] (you) Auto-download on for a podcast, app closed overnight: new episodes are on the phone.
