# M160 Folders as podcasts 3

What [M158](m158.md) left for a book shown as a podcast:

- **Several episodes of one book in Up Next.** Up Next holds a book once, so a second episode of
  the same book takes the place of the first. Queueing three episodes of one show needs a row per
  episode (the key becomes the book and the file, in the table and in sync).
- **Up Next and the book that plays.** An episode queued of the book playing now is left out of
  the service's queue (the loaded book is), and when that book ends the queued episode leaves Up
  Next unplayed.
- **One episode in the player.** An episode Up Next plays alone loads the book's files up to it;
  the player still draws the whole book's timeline, so a seek or a chapter past that episode
  goes nowhere. The timeline should be the episode's, or a seek past it should load the whole book.
- **Android Auto's Up Next shelf** plays a queued episode as its whole book.
- **Skipping while casting**: the service skips played episodes on the receiver when the
  receiver reports going on into the next file; worth a check that every receiver kind (Cast,
  UPnP, FCast) reports that transition, and a fix for those that don't.
- **Sync order**: a sync that brings both a place passed in book view and the switch to podcast
  view can mark episodes the other device left unplayed, since the place is applied first.
