# M148 UI consistency 1

The UI review of 2026-11-20 found the same parts drawn by hand on each screen: titles at four
heights, the amber button written out 15 times, four shapes of empty state. This milestone added
the shared parts in `apps/app/src/components/` and swapped them in on every screen. It changes how
screens look, not what they do, except that a page that fails or runs past 15 seconds now says so
with Try again instead of spinning.

- **`ScreenHeader`**: Back or Close in a 48-point row, an optional action, the `text-3xl` title
  and a subtitle. `SettingsPage` and every pushed screen use it, Transcript and the chapter
  editor included.
- **`Button`** (`primary`, `secondary`, `text`; `md`, `sm`): 48 tall, `busy` for a spinner.
  Disabled is an outline with muted text rather than lower opacity as planned, since the
  accessibility check keeps dimmed text at AA. It replaced every hand-built amber pill, “Set up” on Add-ons and the
  dashed “+ List” on Library; “Add” beside a field is secondary everywhere. `TextButton` is its
  text kind.
- **`EmptyState`** (moved out of `Lori.tsx`): Lori on whole-screen empties, the same type without
  her in sheets and sections.
- **`LoadState`**: loading, error with Try again, content, with a 15-second timeout. Podcast,
  Series, Item, Add-on, Place, the scrobbler page, Discover and Transcript use it. A podcast whose
  page opened by id and isn't in the database, or whose add-on gives no feed, no longer spins
  forever.
- **`Notice`** on theme tokens for the offline line on Library (now through `t()`, with Malay)
  and the headphone battery warning.
- **`TextField`**: one bordered style for every text input. The list's own title, renamed in
  place, stays a bare `TextInput`.

The round play buttons (mini player, Continue card, scrubber) and the car screen's large buttons
stay as they were. Small gaps left in the shared parts are listed in M149. No screenshots were
taken in this milestone; the visual checks are below. The landing page draws Home and the Player,
so run the `site-screenshots` skill after M149.
