# M55 Light landing page

The landing page and developer docs follow the system’s light or dark setting, with a switch in
the header that cycles and remembers system, light or dark. Colours are tokens with both themes in
`apps/site/src/styles/global.css`, checked against WCAG AA in `apps/site/test/contrast.test.ts`;
browsers without `light-dark()` get the night theme. The phone mock-ups keep the app's night
theme, and the app itself is unchanged.

Done when every page reads well in both themes on desktop and phone with no flash of the wrong
theme on load.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) Safari on a Mac and on an iPhone, and Firefox: with a saved theme opposite to the system's,
      a cold load of `/` and `/docs/` never shows the other theme, not even for a frame. With
      JavaScript turned off the page follows the system setting and there is no switch.
- [ ] (you) On a phone at night and by day (system dark mode on and off): the browser bar takes the
      page's colour, the light theme reads well in sunlight outdoors, and the switch is easy to hit.
- [ ] (you) VoiceOver and TalkBack name the switch with its state, “Theme: follows the system” (in Malay
      on `/ms/`, “Tema: ikut sistem”); after a press they read the new state when the focus returns
      to it.
- [ ] (you) Safari 17.4 or older (no `light-dark()`): the whole site shows the night theme and the
      switch is hidden.
