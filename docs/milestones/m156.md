# M156 Add-on protocol 6

Protocol revision 15, asked for by a third-party add-on author on 2026-11-20 so an add-on can
serve older apps differently; the maintainer added a minimum app revision, so listeners are told
to update Lelap. Ships with SDK 0.8. The decision is in the brief under
[The app's revision, a minimum and 426](../brief.md#add-on-protocol).

## What shipped

- **SDK** (`packages/sdk`, `LATEST_PROTOCOL_VERSION` 15): `PROTOCOL_HEADER` (`Lelap-Protocol`),
  `parseAppProtocol`, `sendsAppProtocol`, `needsNewerApp` and the manifest's
  `minProtocolVersion`. `createAddon` hands every handler `appProtocol`, takes
  `minProtocolVersion` and answers `426` with `UPGRADE_MESSAGE` as plain text to an app below it
  (the manifest, `/health` and `/configure` still answer); the preflight sends
  `PREFLIGHT_HEADERS` with `Access-Control-Max-Age: 86400`. `lelap-sdk check` sends the header
  as the app does and warns of a minimum above `protocolVersion`, a minimum without revision 15 and
  a revision 15 preflight that doesn't allow `Lelap-Protocol`. Tests in `revision15.test.ts`;
  the changeset waits for tonight's release.
- **The header** (`packages/core/src/addons/revision.ts`): `protocolHeaders` adds
  `Lelap-Protocol` for an add-on of revision 15 and later on every resource request in
  `client.ts` (catalog, meta, stream, artwork, series), `checkHealth` and `sendScrobbles`, never on
  the manifest. Android's voice search gets the value per catalog (`VoiceCatalog.protocol`,
  `AddonSearch.headers` in `modules/audio`). Add-ons inside the app get `appProtocol` on every
  call through the sandbox (`apps/app/src/sandbox`).
- **Caches by revision:** react-query keys in `apps/app/src/addons.ts`, `CatalogRow`, Search and
  Discover, `SourceCache` (`sourceCacheKey`) and the kept whole series (`series_lists.protocol`,
  migration 0033) all carry `APP_PROTOCOL_VERSION`.
- **A minimum and 426:** `addonBlock` says why an add-on is not asked: its `minProtocolVersion` is
  above the app's revision, or it answered `426` (`AddonRefusals`, kept in the device values under
  `addonRefusals` until the app's revision changes, forgotten when the add-on is removed). A
  blocked add-on is not asked for meta, streams, covers, series, health or scrobbles (events wait);
  its catalog raises `AddonUpgradeError`. The add-on's row in Settings → Library and sources →
  Add-ons and its Home rows show `UpgradeNotice`: “Needs a newer Lelap” or “Update Lelap to use
  {name}” with the add-on's short plain-text note, and Update (`updateLelap` in
  `apps/app/src/updates.ts`: the in-app update on the direct APK, the release page on F-Droid and
  the web). Search shows the same words in place of the add-on's results.
- Docs: “Revision 15” on the protocol page, “The app's revision” on the SDK page, `llms.txt`, the
  brief, and the Malay lines.
