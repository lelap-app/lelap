# M84 Better sound

Listening features people ask for in other players, done on the phone in the playback service.

- Voice boost: a gentle EQ and compression that makes speech clearer in noise.
- Skip silence: shorten long pauses while playing, with the time saved shown.
- Even loudness between books and episodes (loudness normalisation).
- Each per book or per show, with a global default; no effect on casting receivers unless they
  can do it themselves.

Done when each of the three can be turned on per book and is audible on a phone.
