# M84 Better sound

Voice boost, skip silence and even loudness, each a default in Settings → Playback and a choice
of its own per book or per show in Player → Sound. They play on the phone; a cast receiver plays
without them, and the sheet says so. Decision in the [brief](../brief.md#decisions) (“Sound
effects”).

- **Where the choice lives:** like speed. A book's own choice is in `library_entries`, an
  episode of a followed show takes the show's from `subscriptions`; null follows the default.
  Both sync with their row (older devices leave them alone), and so do the three defaults
  (`SYNCED_SETTINGS`). `chooseSound` and `bookSound` in `packages/core/src/sound.ts`; the
  service resolves the same way in `SoundChoice.kt`, so Up Next and Android Auto books started
  with the app closed keep theirs. Migration `0022`.
- **Voice boost:** the M22 voice EQ (and its setting key, `voiceEq`) with a gentle compressor
  after it: 3:1 above -24 dBFS, +6 dB make-up, the limiter last (`Compressor` in `SoundChain.kt`).
  The web adds the same compressor to its Web Audio graph.
- **Skip silence:** Media3's `SilenceSkippingAudioProcessor`, switched with
  `setSkipSilenceEnabled`; pauses over 0.4 s are halved and keep at most 0.75 s (`SilenceRules`).
  `SoundProcessor` reads the processor's skipped-frame count before each flush, so time saved
  survives seeks; `SilenceSavings` keeps it per book on the phone, at the listening speed. The
  sheet shows “12 min saved in this book” once a minute is saved.
- **Even loudness:** ReplayGain album gain, else track gain (and Opus R128 gains), read from ID3,
  Vorbis and MP4 tags as the track loads (`ReplayGain.kt`). Without tags a light measured gain:
  the level of 400 ms blocks with sound in them, averaged over about 10 s, glides towards
  -20 dBFS, within ±10 dB (`Leveller`). A new book is measured afresh.
- **Web:** voice boost only. Skip silence would mean decoding ahead in JavaScript, and even
  loudness without ReplayGain parsing in the browser; [M101](m101.md) has both.

Kotlin JVM tests in `BetterSoundTest.kt` cover choosing the sound, the time-saved accounting,
ReplayGain parsing, the compressor and the leveller; Vitest in `sound.test.ts` covers the
choice, its storage per book and show, and sync.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) Player → Sound for a book shows “For this book” with Voice boost, Skip silence and
      Even loudness; for an episode of a followed show it shows “For this show”. Switching one
      shows “Use the defaults from Settings”, which puts all three back to the Settings values.
- [ ] (adb) Settings → Playback lists Voice boost, Skip silence, Even loudness, Volume boost and
      Mono; changing a default changes the player's switch for a book without its own choice, and
      leaves a book with its own alone.
- [ ] (you) Voice boost: on a muffled LibriVox reader, soft and loud words come closer and
      consonants are clearer, without pumping or hiss; switching while playing doesn't click.
- [ ] (you) Skip silence: on a book with long pauses, pauses shorten without cutting the start or
      end of words; after a few minutes the sheet shows the time saved, which is still there after
      the app restarts. Tune `SilenceRules` by ear if it sounds rushed.
- [ ] (you) Skip silence at 2× and after several seeks: playback and the chapter title stay right,
      and the time saved keeps growing.
- [ ] (you) Even loudness: a quiet and a loud book (or two podcasts) play at about the same level;
      a file tagged with ReplayGain (rsgain or foobar2000) plays at its tag's level from the first
      second; an untagged book settles within a few seconds, with no pumping inside a sentence.
- [ ] (you) A show's skip silence carries to the next episode Up Next starts with the app closed.
- [ ] (you) Casting with all three on: the speaker plays without them, the sheet's note says so,
      and back on the phone they return.
- [ ] (you) A book's own sound and the three defaults reach a second device after a sync; volume
      boost stays on its phone.
- [ ] (you) An hour with all three on costs no noticeable extra battery.
- [ ] (you) Web: voice boost on a download or a LibriVox stream sounds like Android's; the sheet
      leaves out skip silence and even loudness and says they are in the Android app.
- [ ] (you) Malay: a native speaker checks the 15 new lines in `ms.json` (“Penguat suara”,
      “Langkau senyap”, “Kenyaringan sekata”, “Untuk rancangan ini”, the time saved and the notes).
