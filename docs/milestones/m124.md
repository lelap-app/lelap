# M124 Languages 4

What [M99](m99.md) left.

- Chapter names on the notification, the lock screen and Android Auto are fixed when a book loads
  (the playlist and its journal keep them), so after a language switch they stay in the old
  language until the book loads again. The same goes for screens that hold a book's details in
  memory, such as the Continue card. Reload the playing book's chapters on a switch.
- Own chapters made from chapters found from silences show an untitled one as “Chapter 3”, not
  “Part 3”: an own list doesn't say which chapters were found.
- An SMB or SFTP failure wrapped in another exception gives “The download failed.” rather than its
  own line: `Reasons.of` doesn't know `NetFailure`, which lives in `modules/netfs`.
- A few internal failures with English text remain, though they are never shown as they are
  (casting's “Unknown kind of receiver”, saving a download job).
- Chapter names the listener typed: migration 0027 (M99) also emptied own chapter titles that read
  exactly “Chapter n”, “Bab n”, “Part n” or “Bahagian n” for their place, so a typed “Bab 3” now
  shows as “Chapter 3” in an English profile. Keep names the listener typed, and only name empty
  ones. (Found while preparing beta 11.)
