# M109 Series and reading order

Series are worked out on the device from what the library already knows, then shown, ordered and
followed. Decision: [Series](../brief.md#decisions) in the brief.

**Where a series comes from.** Every source writes it the same way, `Dune #1, Dune Chronicles #1`,
in the first file's `kind_tags.series`, so one reader serves all of them (`parseSeries` in
`packages/core/src/library/seriesNames.ts`):

- Audiobookshelf's series name, as since [M65](m65.md).
- The files' own tags (`modules/library`, `IdTags.kt`): `SERIES` and `SERIES-PART` in ID3 `TXXX`,
  MP4 `----` items and Vorbis comments, or the movement name and number (`MVNM`/`MVIN`,
  `©mvn`/`©mvi`) that tagging apps use for audiobooks. `filesNeedingTags` reads the first file of
  each book once more (`readsSeries`) when it was read before the reader knew series.
- An add-on's meta for a streamed book (`addStreamedBook`), and the work a book was matched with
  through Match with catalog… (`LinkedMeta.series`, synced with the link), which wins over the tags.

**Grouping and order** (`packages/core/src/library/series.ts`). Names compare folded, without a
leading article or a trailing “series”. Two names also become one series when the SDK's work
matcher finds the same work at the same number under both (a catalog's “Expanse, The” and a
server's “The Expanse”), and the same work twice in one series is listed once. Books go by number,
fractional numbers between whole ones (2.5 after 2), books without a number last, then by title.
Whole numbers from 1 to the highest that no book has are the gaps (up to 100, so a year is no gap).
The listener can put a series in an order of their own; it is kept as book keys in the setting
`seriesOrder:<key>` and syncs as the `seriesOrder` operation, which older versions skip.

**In the app.**

- Library → Series (`apps/app/app/(tabs)/library.tsx`, `SeriesRow`): every series with its author,
  count, finished books and gaps.
- A series page (`apps/app/app/series.tsx`): books in reading order with their number, each gap
  as “Book 2 is not in your library.” with Look for it, and Change the order (up and down) or
  Order by number.
- The player names the series as a link to its page, and when a book has ended (or is finished and
  paused) shows Next in the series (`NextInSeries`): Play next book when the library has it,
  else Look in your add-ons for the next number.
- Look for it and Look in your add-ons (`findSeriesVolume`, `packages/core/src/addons/seriesVolume.ts`)
  search the add-ons' search catalogs for the series' name and author, read the meta of up to 8
  results and open the page of the one whose meta names that series at that number. Nothing is
  asked until the listener taps.
- An item page whose work names a series the library has links to it (“3 books of Dune in your
  library”).
