# M73 Landing page polish

Findings on the site (`apps/site`) left for later.

- The theme switch’s new name may not be announced after a press (needs `aria-live` or a tooltip), and the icon-only button has no tooltip for mouse users. (M55)
- Other open tabs keep the old theme until they reload (no `storage` listener). (M55)
- `.visually-hidden` in `Base.astro` repeats `.lang-name` and the manifest builder’s copy; make it one shared class in `global.css`. (M55)
- At 320 px the header wraps to two rows and the hero’s phone mock-up is wider than the screen. (M55)
- The manifest builder’s input borders are below 3:1 contrast in both themes. (M55)
- The landing page fills `{holes}` into `set:html` lines without escaping; escape them before a hole holds anything but configuration. (M48)

Done when the site passes its contrast test at 320 px and the switch is announced.
