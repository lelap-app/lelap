# M102 Add-on protocol 4

What [M66](m66.md) left out, because it needs a new control, native work or a bigger change.

- The app sends no `rate`: there is no rating control, and nowhere in the app to keep or show a
  rating. (M30)
- `finish` events on Android carry no `sessionStartMs`; the playback service could add it to its
  end-of-book log. Native work in `modules/audio`. (M51)
- The first-party scrobble add-ons seal their own settings; they could move to the SDK's
  `configure` once it covers their OAuth flows. (M27)
- Look up Goodreads books missing from the library through the Catalog add-on, for a Want to read
  list. (M34)
- The LibriVox catalog rates from archive.org's `avg_rating` and the item page from the item's
  reviews; they can differ by a few tenths. Matching them needs a request per tile or a rating
  kept from the item page. (M49)
- Release builds set no `EXPO_PUBLIC_LELAP_CONTACT`, so the reading log's User-Agent has no contact
  address yet; it needs a repository variable in `release.yml`. (M61)
- Editions come from the Open Library catalog only; Hardcover and Audnexus know editions too.
