# M138 Playback history

A pocket tap, a headphone double-press or a slip on the scrubber can move a book hours away, and
the listener can't tell where they were. Each book keeps a short log on the device of what moved
its place, and one tap goes back.

## What is logged

One entry per action, with the time, the place before and after, and where it came from (app,
notification, lock screen, headphones, widget, car, watch): play, pause, skip back and forward,
a seek (scrubber, “Jump to”, a bookmark, a shared place), a chapter jump, and the sleep timer's
stop. Several skips in a row within a few seconds become one entry, from the first place to the
last; dragging the scrubber is one entry, on release. Auto-advance between files, smart rewind
and the service's own seeks aren't logged, the same line [M86](m86.md) draws for “last used”.

It builds on M86: the sleep timer's stop and the last use before it become two entries of the
log, and “Back to when you last used it” reads them instead of `SleepPlace.kt`'s own record. The
offer itself stays as it is.

## Where it shows

- **Player.** After a skip, seek or chapter jump of more than 1 minute, the existing `UndoBar`
  shows “Moved from 1:02:13. Go back” for 10 s. A “History” entry in the player's menu lists
  the book's log, newest first, with clock time, the action and the place (“23:41 · Skipped
  forward from 1:02:13 to 1:32:13 · headphones”). A tap moves the place there without playing,
  as M86's choices do.
- **Notification and car.** Accidental jumps mostly happen away from the screen, so for 30 s
  after a jump that didn't come from the app, the notification's custom button becomes “Back”
  (a Media3 custom command), and Android Auto gets the same command. Then the button returns to
  what it was. Whether to take one of the notification's few slots is checked on a phone first;
  if it crowds the controls, the notification part is dropped.
- **Web.** The web player logs its own controls and the browser's media keys (Media Session
  actions) and its sleep timer's stop, and shows the same undo bar and list. It has no
  notification button.

## Kept and not kept

- **Where:** a table in the device's database (`packages/core/src/schema.ts`, migration made with
  `drizzle-kit generate`), per profile and book. On Android the playback service records actions
  while the app sleeps, in a small buffer the app collects when it wakes, as finished items are
  collected today (`takeFinished`).
- **How long:** the last 50 entries per book, none older than 30 days. Removing a book removes
  its log.
- **Not synced.** The log is about the last minutes on this device: an undo on the phone for a
  jump made on the web would surprise more than help, and syncing every pause would add an
  oplog entry per tap. Like the listening log, it stays on the device (see “Listening stats” in
  the [brief](../brief.md#decisions)); the place itself still syncs as today. The decision goes
  into the brief with the build.
- **Pure logic** (coalescing, the 1-minute threshold, keeping 50 and 30 days, what counts) goes
  in `packages/core` with Vitest tests; the service and the web player only report actions.
