# M138 Playback history

A pocket tap, a headphone double-press or a slip on the scrubber can move a book hours away, and
the listener can't tell where they were. Each book keeps a short log on the device of what moved
its place, and one tap goes back. Decision in the brief:
[Playback history](../brief.md#decisions).

- **The log** is `playback_history` in `packages/core/src/schema.ts` (migration `0030`), per
  profile and book, with the action, its source, the time and the whole-book place before and
  after. `packages/core/src/history.ts` holds the rules: a run of skips from one source within
  5 s is one entry from the first place to the last; the last 50 entries per book, none older
  than 30 days; a removed book or profile takes its log along. Not synced.
- **What is logged:** play, pause, skip, seek (scrubber, “Jump to”, a bookmark, a shared place,
  the history itself), chapter jump, setting or changing the sleep timer, and the timer's stop.
  Auto-advance, smart rewind and the service's own seeks aren't. Speed changes aren't either, so
  they no longer count as “last used” for M86.
- **Who logs it:** the app logs its own taps (`apps/app/src/history.ts`), since it knows what they
  were. The Android service logs what comes from elsewhere into a small buffer the app takes when
  it looks (`PlaybackHistory.kt`, `takeHistory`): the notification and lock screen, headphones,
  the car, a watch, the widget, a shake and the timer's stop. It tells sources apart by the
  controller; the app's own controller says so in its connection hints. The web player logs its
  media keys (Media Session actions) and its timer's stop the same way (`audio.web.ts`).
- **Player:** after a skip, seek or chapter jump of more than 1 minute, the `UndoBar` says “Moved
  from 1:02:13.” with “Go back” for 10 s, also for a jump from headphones or the notification
  while the player is open. A History tool lists the book's log, newest first (“23:41 · Skipped
  forward from 1:02:13 to 1:32:13 · headphones”); a tap goes to the place before that action
  without starting to play (`HistorySheet.tsx`).
- **Notification and car:** for 30 s after a jump of more than 1 minute made away from the app,
  the notification's sleep timer button becomes “Back to where you were” (a Media3 custom command,
  `GO_BACK`), which Android Auto shows too; then the sleep button comes back (`BackOffer` in
  `PlaybackHistory.kt`). A run of skips counts from its first place.
- **M86:** “Back to when you last used it” reads the timer's stop and the last use before it from
  the log (`sleepStopOf`); `SleepPlace.kt` is gone. The offer now shows on the web as well. “Not
  now” is kept on the device per stop.

## Open checks

- [ ] (adb) Play a book, press the notification's forward button three times quickly with a 30 s
      skip interval: the player's History shows one “Skipped forward” entry from the first place to
      the last, with “notification”.
- [ ] (adb) With the app closed, skip forward with the notification five times (2.5 min): the
      sleep timer button turns into “Back to where you were” at once; pressing it returns to the
      first place and the sleep button comes back. Without pressing, it goes back to the sleep
      button after 30 s.
- [ ] (adb) A single 30 s skip from the notification, or a skip in the app, leaves the sleep timer
      button as it is.
- [ ] (adb) In the app, tap a chapter in the chapter list two chapters on: the bar says “Moved from
      …” with “Go back”; Go back returns to the place and the bar doesn't come back for it.
- [ ] (adb) Open History, tap an older entry while paused: the place moves there and nothing plays.
- [ ] (adb) A sleep timer stops playback: History shows “Sleep timer stopped at …”, and the player
      offers “Back to when you last used it” with the last press before it (also when the press came
      from the notification while the app was closed). “Not now” hides it, also after reopening the
      app.
- [ ] (adb) Switch profile after skipping with the notification: the skips are in the first
      profile's History, not the second's.
- [ ] (adb) Remove a book from the library: its history is gone (`adb shell run-as app.lelap
  sqlite3` on the database, or History after adding it again).
- [ ] (you) Web app in Chrome: the keyboard's media keys and the browser's media controls skip and
      pause; History shows them as “media keys”.
- [ ] (you) [car] In Android Auto, skip forward several times: the car shows “Back to where you
      were” for 30 s, and pressing it goes back.
- [ ] (you) Bluetooth headphones and a watch: their skips and pauses show in History as
      “headphones” and “watch”.
- [ ] (you) Whether “Back to where you were” crowds the notification's controls on your phone; if
      it does, the notification part goes (the car keeps it).
- [ ] (you) A native speaker checks the new Malay lines in `apps/app/src/i18n/catalogs/ms.json`:
      “Sejarah”, “Kembali”, “Kembali ke tempat tadi”, “Dialih dari {place}.”, “Dimajukan dari {from}
      ke {to}”, “Diundur dari {from} ke {to}”, “Tukar bab dari {from} ke {to}”, “Pemasa tidur
      berhenti pada {place}” and the source words (“fon kepala”, “pemberitahuan”, “kekunci media”).
