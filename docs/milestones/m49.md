# M49 Artwork badges

Protocol revision 4 adds `badges` (a rating, new episodes) to catalog items and metas
(`packages/sdk`). The app draws them with its own finished and progress badges on every cover, in
one style, read as part of the cover's name, with a switch in Settings → Catalogs
(`packages/core/src/badges.ts`). The LibriVox add-on rates its covers from archive.org reviews,
from three reviews up (`services/catalog`).

Done when a catalog add-on can show a rating on its covers and the listener can turn badges off.
