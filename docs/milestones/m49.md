# M49 Artwork badges

**Status:** in progress. Protocol revision 4 adds `badges` (a rating, new episodes) to catalog
items and metas; the app draws them with its own finished and progress badges on every cover, in
one style, read as part of the cover's name, with a switch in Settings. The LibriVox add-on rates
its covers from archive.org reviews. Phone checks are in [m49-follow-ups](../m49-follow-ups.md).

The app draws badges on covers (rating, new episodes, finished) from data add-ons give with their
catalog items and metas, instead of add-ons baking text into images.

## Done when

- A catalog add-on can show a rating on its covers and the listener can turn badges off.
