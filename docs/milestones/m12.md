# M12 Look and feel

One SVG icon set (`apps/app/src/components/Icon.tsx`) replaces the text glyphs; the Player follows
the design sketch (skip-30 icons, bookmark button by the title, book progress under the scrubber,
a tool row for speed, sleep, chapters and bookmarks). Home shows the add-on count and a “Ready for
offline” card, Search says how many add-ons answered, and the Library shows a banner when offline
(`expo-network`). Alerts go through `showAlert`, which draws an in-app dialog on the web. Covers
fade in, the mini player slides up and the play button gives under the finger; nothing moves with
reduced motion.

Done when every screen matches the sketch's look on a phone and on the web.
