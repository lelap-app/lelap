# M107 Car and Bluetooth 3

The leftovers from [M74](m74.md), until now under Future goals in the roadmap.

- **Car mode sideways on phones** (`apps/app/app/car.tsx`): the app stays upright, but car mode
  lifts the lock while it is open (`useSideways` in `apps/app/src/platform/orientation.ts`, with
  `expo-screen-orientation`) and puts it back on the way out; the phone's auto-rotate setting
  still applies. Sideways, the cover, title, chapter and bar share the left side, and the buttons
  keep a column of their own on the right, with Exit at the top. The cover takes what the text
  and a headphone warning leave, in both shapes. At night the cover's colours tint the whole
  screen faintly (a blurred copy at 18 %, so the text keeps 5:1); by day the paper stays plain.
- **Covers per car app:** `CoverProvider` in `modules/audio` is no longer exported. When a car app
  asks for a list or an item, the playback service grants that app read access to the covers in
  it (`CarCovers.grants`); Lelap itself and a controller whose package Android hides get nothing.
  The provider still serves only covers of books the car lists.
- **Headphone drain once per session** (`packages/core/src/headphones.ts`, `apps/app/src/devices.tsx`):
  each device's stored value is now the drain learned from finished sessions plus the levels of
  the open one. A pause ends the session and folds it in once. After an app restart, a session
  whose last level is under 90 min old and whose battery didn't rise goes on; an older one is
  folded in then. The plain number stored before reads as the learned drain.

      camera cut-out covers nothing. Exit: the app is upright again.

      cover's colours tint the background faintly; by day the background is plain paper.

      after leaving car mode sideways.
- [ ] (adb) `dumpsys package app.lelap | grep -A3 covers` (or `content read`): the cover provider is
      not exported, and `content read --uri content://app.lelap.covers/<key>` from the shell is
      refused.

- [ ] (you) [car] Car mode sideways on a phone in a landscape holder, by day and by night: title and
