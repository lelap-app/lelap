# M58 Navidrome and Subsonic

**Status:** planned.

A bundled in-app add-on (M57) for servers that speak the Subsonic API: Navidrome, Airsonic,
Gonic and others, where many people keep audiobooks next to their music. Token login, the
library scanned as books, streaming and downloads, and places kept with the server's bookmarks
where it has them.

## Done when

- A Navidrome server's audiobooks show in the library, play and download, and a place saved in
  Lelap shows on the server's bookmark.
