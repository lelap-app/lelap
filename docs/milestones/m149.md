# M149 UI consistency 2

The second half of the UI review of 2026-11-20, built on M148's shared parts: what the listener
sees and reads. Decisions are in the brief (Settings, Tabs and rail, Type scale, Cover badges).

- **Tab header:** `TabHeader` on all five tabs: the title on a 48-point row at `insets.top + 12`,
  the tab's own buttons, then a gear for Settings last (the `settings` icon is a gear now, here
  and on the landing page). Home keeps Stats and the profile switch; Podcasts has Up next as an
  icon. The add-on count left Home for Settings › Library and sources › Add-ons.
- **Desktop rail:** `src/components/Rail.tsx`, drawn by the root layout beside every screen
  except the welcome, car mode and the full player; the tabs draw no bar of their own on a wide
  screen. 96 points wide (`RAIL_WIDTH`), labels on up to two lines, the lists, and Settings at its
  foot. The tab under a pushed screen stays lit; from a pushed screen a tab is reached with
  `dismissTo`, so a second set of tabs is never pushed.
- **Settings rows:** `LinkRow` for rows that open another screen (Help › Diagnostics, People and
  devices › Profiles and Sync, Library and sources › Add-ons, Downloads and storage › Downloads),
  each with its state as the detail. A `TextButton` inside a row sits flush with its end.
- **Type scale:** `title`, `heading`, `section`, `body`, `detail` and `caption` in
  `apps/app/tailwind.config.js`; every Tailwind size, `text-[10px]` and car mode's inline sizes
  moved onto it, and `scripts/check-type-scale.mjs` (in `pnpm test`) fails on any other.
  Drawings that size letters to themselves (initials, the shared stats image) are listed there.
  Look and language and Listening use the small-caps `SectionHeader`, which can carry an
  information button; the sound paragraph has its own heading.
- **Names:** “Stats” for the stats page and its button; Downloads and storage's row is
  “Downloads”, like the screen it opens; “Sleep timer” in the player, car mode and Settings;
  Transcript says book, part or episode; “podcast” instead of “show”; “Long-press”; the Year page
  is “Your year” (a past year shows its number).
- **Copy:** the “Metadata only” badge is gone (`SourceBadge` draws nothing for an item without
  audio); “Paste a podcast’s feed address”, “Bring podcasts from another app”, “Save your podcasts
  as a file”; “Up next” in sentence case, also in Android Auto; “0%” everywhere; an offline book
  in the Library says “Needs a connection”.
- **Home and Library:** the Library's Continue listening card is gone; on a wide screen Home
  hides its card while the player pane shows the same book.
- **Polish:** a chosen accent keeps its dot inside an accent ring; the theme file is two rows;
  the Sleep timer sheet has one moon, by its title (`Sheet` takes an `icon`); Stats' weeks show
  the day with the month under the first week of each month; short months are three letters
  (`locale.ts`, “Sep”, not “Sept”); lists with a rule use the `line` token; the scrubber's times
  have names on the web (“12:34 played”, “40:00 left”). The player's cover is not a button in the
  current code, so it needed no name.
- **M148's gaps:** `Button` takes a `detail` line (the sleep stop banner uses it); `TextField`
  has `invalid` (chapter editor), `locked` (server form) and `digits` (the PIN);
  `ScreenHeader inGap` drops its bottom space where the screen's own gap gives it (Diagnostics,
  Place, Scrobbler, Sync, Server); `confirmAddon` takes `onFetched`, so the add-on link's
  `LoadState` times out the fetch and not the question; `useSeriesList` returns `retry`, which
  the Series page's Try again calls.
- `site-screenshots` clicks “Sleep timer”; the landing page's Home drawing lost the add-on pill
  and the “Metadata only” badges.
