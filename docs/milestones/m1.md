# M1 Audio spike

A Kotlin Expo module in `modules/audio` around a Media3 `MediaLibraryService`: gapless parts,
playback with the screen off and the app closed, lock screen and headphone controls, speed that
keeps pitch, ducking on calls (a setting), and a sleep timer that fades the player's own volume
over the last 60 seconds. The test screen is Settings → Developer → Audio spike
(`apps/app/app/dev/spike.tsx`); results so far are in
[`modules/audio/README.md`](../../modules/audio/README.md#m1-device-checklist). Design:
[`superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-m1-audio-spike-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-m1-audio-spike-design.md).
[#89](https://github.com/lelap-app/lelap/pull/89) made Play during a call duck instead of pause.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) With “Duck on calls” on, press Play during a phone call and during a VoIP call: the book
      plays quietly at once (it paused at once in `v0.1.0-beta.3`), and at full volume when the call
      ends. With it off: “starts when the call ends”, then it starts. Passed on the emulator on
      2026-11-17 (Android 17, a faked phone call: −14 dB during the call, 0 dB after); still to do on
      a phone. Known: a book already playing when a call rings is muted by Android itself until the
      call ends (“muted player piids due to call/ring” in `dumpsys audio`), so it is silent rather
      than ducked.
- [ ] (you) Audio spike screen, with the switch on: a real phone call and a WhatsApp call duck the book.
      With the switch off, both pause it, and it resumes after the call.
- [ ] (you) Audio spike screen: with the screen off for five minutes and the app swiped away, headphone
      play, pause and next work.
- [ ] (you) Audio spike screen: pause during a 1-minute sleep timer; the countdown freezes while paused,
      and the next Play starts at normal volume.
