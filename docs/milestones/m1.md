# M1 Audio spike

A Kotlin Expo module in `modules/audio` around a Media3 `MediaLibraryService`: gapless parts,
playback with the screen off and the app closed, lock screen and headphone controls, speed that
keeps pitch, ducking on calls (a setting), and a sleep timer that fades the player's own volume
over the last 60 seconds. The test screen is Settings → Developer → Audio spike
(`apps/app/app/dev/spike.tsx`); results so far are in
[`modules/audio/README.md`](../../modules/audio/README.md#m1-device-checklist). Design:
[`superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-m1-audio-spike-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-m1-audio-spike-design.md).
[#89](https://github.com/lelap-app/lelap/pull/89) made Play during a call duck instead of pause.

Settings → Playback → During calls now chooses Pause or Lower the volume, with the level from 20 to
100 % in steps of 10 (20 % by default, the old fixed level). The old switch kept its key: on is
lowering to 20 %, off is Pause.

      its volume (`dumpsys audio` shows the player's volume at 0.5) and comes back to full when the
      call ends. Moving the slider during the call changes the level at once. With Pause: playback
      pauses for the call and resumes when it ends.
