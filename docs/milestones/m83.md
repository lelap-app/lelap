# M83 Accessibility 2

A second pass over the whole app, after [M42](m42.md). The source check,
`apps/app/scripts/check-accessibility.mjs` (run by `pnpm --filter @lelap/app test`), now finds
whole kinds of problems instead of one screen at a time, and what it found is fixed:

- **Names and states.** A checkbox, radio, switch or tab without its state fails, and so does a
  control that turns amber when chosen without saying so. A button that shows a spinner in place
  of its text keeps its name (Play on the item page, Subscribe on a podcast, Play on a shared
  place). Sheets and the web's dialogs are named by their title, so a screen reader says which
  dialog it entered.
- **Headings.** Every screen has one; the Player and car mode now read the book's title as a
  heading. The add-on link screen is a passing screen with one line, read as it changes.
- **Errors are announced.** Text that appears when something fails is a live region: search
  sections, the item page's sources, the podcast feed, the scrobbler, casting, the Player.
- **Large text.** Text in a fixed height it outgrows at font scale 2 fails; Settings' bedtime −
  and + keep their glyphs within the round button. The handoff banner wraps its buttons below the
  question instead of cutting it off.
- **Targets.** Text fields (Podcasts, Sync, Profiles, a server, a scrobbler, Convert, the Player's
  Jump to and bookmark note, a new list's name) are at least 48 points tall, and short buttons such as “Add” and “Use”, chips and
  the Player's elapsed time at least 48 wide. Every switch goes through `Toggle`, which on the web
  is 48 by 24 with a muted track that turns amber, instead of the browser's faint 40 by 20.
- **Contrast.** Dimming with opacity fails, since it takes text below AA: finished episodes and
  books that can't play here are dimmed with `text-muted` instead, and only the cover fades. A
  colour outside the theme fails unless its pair is checked: the offline and headphone banners,
  the rule-list icon, a profile's initial on its avatar tile (now ink on light tiles; cream was
  1.7:1 on amber), and both palettes of car mode and its stats, day and night. Icon colours in
  `Icon.tsx` must match `tailwind.config.js`.
- **The web at 320 px.** Car mode shrinks its three round buttons to fit side by side (the skips
  stay 70 points); leaving it before the browser grants the wake lock no longer throws. The tab
  bar is 4 points taller on the web, so the labels' descenders show.
