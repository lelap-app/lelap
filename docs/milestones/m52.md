# M52 Sync upkeep

**Status:** in progress. Built: sealed snapshots that replace messages older than 30 days, new devices joining from them, and idle groups expiring by the `SYNC_IDLE_EXPIRY_DAYS` setting (730 days on lelap.app). Open checks are in [`m52-follow-ups.md`](../m52-follow-ups.md).

The sync log stops growing forever: a device writes a sealed snapshot and the server drops the operations before it, and groups idle for a long time expire. From [`m7-follow-ups.md`](../m7-follow-ups.md) and [`m19-follow-ups.md`](../m19-follow-ups.md).

## Done when

- A group used daily for a year stays well under the 50 MB cap, and a new device joins from the
  snapshot instead of replaying the whole log.
