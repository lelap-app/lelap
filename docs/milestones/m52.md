# M52 Sync upkeep

A device folds a group's messages older than 30 days into a sealed snapshot once they reach 1 MB,
the server drops the messages it covers, and new devices join from the snapshot (`packages/core`
sync engine, `services/sync` with migration `0002_snapshots.sql`). Groups no device has pushed to
expire by the `SYNC_IDLE_EXPIRY_DAYS` setting, run by a daily cron trigger; the Docker store runs
the same SQL. How snapshots and expiry work is in the [brief](../brief.md#sync).

Done when a group used daily for a year stays well under the 50 MB cap, and a new device joins from
the snapshot instead of replaying the whole log.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) After a staging deploy,
      `wrangler d1 execute lelap-sync-staging --remote --command "SELECT id, last_push_at, snapshot_seq FROM groups"`
      shows dates from the last push, and the Worker's cron trigger appears in the dashboard.
      (`SYNC_IDLE_EXPIRY_DAYS=730` is set in `release` and went out with v0.1.0-beta.5; staging
      leaves `SYNC_STAGING_IDLE_EXPIRY_DAYS` unset to keep its groups.)
- [ ] (you) Two devices on staging, with over 1 MB of sync messages older than 30 days (set the phone's
      clock, or wait): the next sync writes a snapshot; a newly paired device gets the whole library.
- [ ] (you) An app from before M52 that missed a snapshot shows the sync failure with HTTP 410 in Settings
      → Diagnostics, keeps its library, and syncs again after updating.
- [ ] (you) Docker: `docker compose up sync` with `SYNC_IDLE_EXPIRY_DAYS=1` deletes a group untouched for
      a day at the next start.
