# M147 Play by voice

In a car, typing is not an option, and Android Auto expects a media app to answer voice search.
`PlaybackService` is a Media3 `MediaLibraryService` but handles no search yet.

- Answer `onSearch` and `onGetSearchResult` from the library and downloads first, then the
  search catalogs of installed add-ons, books before episodes.
- A request with one clear match plays it (“play Dune”, “play the latest episode of X”); several
  matches show as a list in Android Auto. “Resume my book” plays what was last played.
- Offline, search only what is on the phone; never wait on an add-on for longer than Auto allows.

**Done when** “Hey Google, play Pride and Prejudice on Lelap” starts the book from its place,
in the car and on the phone, and an unclear request lists the matches in Android Auto.
