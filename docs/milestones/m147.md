# M147 Play by voice

“Hey Google, play Pride and Prejudice on Lelap” and Android Auto's voice and search buttons are
answered by `PlaybackService` itself, so they work with the app closed. Decision in
[`docs/brief.md`](../brief.md) (Play by voice).

- **Reading the request** (`VoiceQuery.kt`): filler words (“play”, “on Lelap”, “the audiobook”,
  “podcast”) go; nothing, the app's name or “resume my book” resumes; “the latest episode of X”
  asks for a show's newest episode; “X by Y” names the author. The Assistant's own title and
  artist extras win when it sends them.
- **Ranking** (`VoiceSearch.kt`): whole titles, a title's start, shared words and authors score
  0–100; the phone before add-ons, books before episodes, the one played last among equals. A
  match of 85 or more, ten ahead of the next, is clear.
- **On the phone** (`VoiceLibrary.kt`): the car snapshot's shelves and lists, the journal's book
  and up to 60 more unfinished library books (`library`, `carShelves` in `packages/core/src/car.ts`),
  with `episodes` marking which are episodes. No network needed.
- **Add-ons** (`AddonSearch.kt`): with no clear match and a validated network, the audiobook search
  catalogs of the web add-ons the profile sees (`voiceCatalogs`), all at once, for at most three
  seconds. In-app add-ons are left out: their sandbox can't start in time.
- **Playing**: `onSetMediaItems` takes the search query Media3 hands over for play-from-search; a
  phone match plays like a book picked in the car, from its place. An add-on item goes to the app's
  new `play` server task (`ServerTasks.PLAY`, `apps/app/src/background.ts`), which adds it as its
  Play button would and plays it. From the phone's Assistant with the app closed, that can come
  after Android's grace for starting a foreground service, so it may play without its
  notification; see the open checks. Nothing found sends Android Auto “Nothing found for …”.
- **The phone's Assistant**: `VoiceSearchActivity` takes `MEDIA_PLAY_FROM_SEARCH`, shows nothing
  and hands the query to the service, which answers it the same way.
- **Android Auto's list**: `onSearch` answers at once and tells Auto when the matches are in;
  `onGetSearchResult` lists the phone's matches, then the add-ons' (without copies of books
  already in the library).

Kotlin unit tests: `VoiceQueryTest`, `VoiceSearchTest`, `VoiceLibraryTest`, `AddonSearchTest`.

## Open checks

- [ ] (you) [car] In Android Auto with the app closed, say “Play Pride and Prejudice on Lelap” for
      a book in the library: it starts from its saved place within a few seconds.
- [ ] (you) [car] In Android Auto, say “Play Jane Austen on Lelap” with two of her books in the
      library: one plays, and “more results” (or the search button) lists both.
- [ ] (you) [car] In Android Auto, say “Resume my book on Lelap”: the book played last resumes.
- [ ] (you) [car] In Android Auto, say “Play the latest episode of <a followed show> on Lelap”:
      its newest unfinished episode plays.
- [ ] (you) [car] In Android Auto, ask for a book that is not in the library but in LibriVox: after
      a few seconds the app adds it and it plays; picking an add-on result in the search list does
      the same.
- [ ] (you) [car] With mobile data and Wi-Fi off, ask in Android Auto for a downloaded book: it
      plays at once, and a request nothing on the phone matches says “Nothing found for …” without
      a long wait.
- [ ] (you) On the phone with the screen locked, “Hey Google, play Pride and Prejudice on Lelap”
      starts the book from its place.
- [ ] (you) With the app closed, “Hey Google, play <a LibriVox book not in the library> on Lelap”
      on the phone: it is added and plays, with the player's notification showing. If the
      notification is missing, the `play` server task needs a foreground start of its own.
- [ ] (you) With Lelap open in Recents, “Hey Google, play Pride and Prejudice on Lelap”: the app's
      screen does not come to the front.
- [ ] (adb) With the app closed and a book “Pride and Prejudice” in the library, run
      `adb shell am start -a android.media.action.MEDIA_PLAY_FROM_SEARCH -e query "Pride and Prejudice"`
      and pick Lelap if asked: nothing shows, and the book plays from its place within a few
      seconds (`adb logcat` shows no crash of `PlaybackService`).
