# M127 Series 3

A series page knew only the books in the library ([M109](m109.md), [M115](m115.md)) and showed
gaps up to the highest number it had. Now it shows the whole series, from Wikidata's open data
(CC0, no key), so a listener sees every Harry Potter or Department Q book in order and what each is
called. Decision: [Series](../brief.md#decisions) in the brief.

- **Protocol: the `series` resource** (revision 11, with SDK 0.6; `.changeset/m127-protocol-series.md`).
  `GET /series/audiobook/{name}/{extra}.json` with `author`, `language` and `work` (an `ol:` work of
  the series). The answer, `SeriesResponse`, is `{ series: { name, id, link, volumes } | null }`; a
  book has a `title`, `titles` by language, a `position` (`1`, `0.5`), a `released` year or date
  and `externalIds`. `seriesPath`, `offersSeries`, `orderSeriesVolumes` and a `series` handler in
  `createAddon` (`packages/sdk/src`). `lelap-sdk check` asks a name no series has and, with
  `--series name/author`, one it should know.
- **The Whole series add-on** (`services/catalog/src/series/addon.ts`, `/series/manifest.json`,
  `app.lelap.series`), a default add-on of the app. An add-on of its own, not a resource of the
  Open Library catalog: an app before revision 11 refuses a manifest that lists a resource it
  doesn't know, and would lose the Catalog. `services/catalog/src/wikidata.ts` looks the name up
  with `wbsearchentities`, then reads the candidates' written works (P179 with the P1545 ordinal;
  P31 literary work, book, novel, novella, short story and the like, so the TV series, its seasons
  and the films never count) with four small SPARQL queries; one big UNION ran past the query
  service's time limit. The candidate wins whose books are by the asked author (P50); an Open
  Library work (P648) settles it when it is in one series. Titles in the asked language, else the
  original language (P407), else English. Through `upstream.json`, so the Cache API on Workers and
  the memory cache in Docker keep each answer for a week. Wikidata's answers for both series are
  recorded in `services/catalog/test/fixtures/wikidata-answers.json`.
- **Order** (`orderSeriesVolumes`): by position; a book without one after the last numbered book
  released no later than it; books with neither last. The listener's own order (M109) still wins.
  Half numbers (Harry Potter's 0.5 prequel) and books without a number (The Cursed Child) are
  listed in their place but never counted as missing or offered as the next book: short stories
  and companions a reading order can do without.
- **Kept on the device** (`packages/core/src/library/seriesList.ts`, table `series_lists`,
  migration 0029): the last answer per series and language, asked again after 30 days (3 when no
  add-on knew it), never synced. The page works offline from it.
- **Series page** (`apps/app/app/series.tsx`, `seriesRows`): every listed book in order. A library
  book is matched by its whole title first, then by number, then by a title without subtitle;
  one the list lacks (Department Q book 10) goes by its number. A missing book shows its title,
  number and year with Look for it, which searches the add-ons by its titles and the author
  (`findSeriesVolume`), preferring a work whose meta names the series at that number. Missing
  titles follow the language of the editions the library has, so Danish books get Danish titles.
- **Next in the series** (`nextInWholeSeries`, `NextInSeries.tsx`) names the next book by title
  when the library lacks it, or plays the next one it has.
- **What Wikidata had on 2026-11-19.** Harry Potter (Q8337): books 1 to 7 with ordinals, a prequel
  at 0.5 (2008) and The Cursed Child without one; book 7 has no date. Department Q (Q86567441,
  “Afdeling Q”): books 1 to 9 with ordinals and Danish titles; only 1, 2 and 4 have a year. Book
  10 (2024) is not in Wikidata, so a library copy of it shows after book 9 by its own number.
- **Checked in the web app** with the catalog service running locally against Wikidata: Department
  Q with books 1, 2, 4 and 10 in Danish lists 3 and 5 to 9 by their Danish titles; Harry Potter with
  books 1 and 3 lists the prequel at 0.5, 2 and 4 to 7, and the Cursed Child last, “5 missing”;
  Look for it on Chamber of Secrets opened Open Library's work; with the service stopped the page
  showed the kept list; an existing web library picked up the new default add-on on start.
