# M15 Find any book

The item page asks every add-on with a `stream` resource for the work, by its own ID or one of its
`externalIds` (`findSources` in `packages/core/src/addons/sources.ts`). It lists each source with
its add-on, length and parts, and remembers the choice per book and profile in the `settings`
table under `source:<type>:<id>`, on the device only. A work without audio gets a page of its own
with description, tags, a link to its source and a way to Settings → Add-ons.
