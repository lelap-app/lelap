# M30 Scrobbling

**Status:** in progress (PR open). `POST /scrobble` in the app, Hardcover and gPodder add-ons, a "Want to read" row on Home. Details: [`next.md`](../next.md).

- **Protocol:** `scrobble` is a resource; `POST /scrobble` takes `{ events: ScrobbleEvent[] }`
  (start, progress, pause, finish, abandon, rate; each with an `eventId` and its real `at`; never
  a source, stream or file URL). Catalogs may carry `shelf: "want-to-read"`. All additions are
  optional fields, so existing add-ons stay valid. `createAddon` takes a `scrobble` handler.
- **Core:** `scrobble_outbox` (migration 0011) holds events per add-on; `sendScrobbles` posts them
  in order, deletes on 2xx or a permanent 4xx, backs off from 30 s to 6 h otherwise, drops events
  after 30 days or when the add-on is removed. `ScrobbleTracker` turns player states into start,
  progress (every 5 min), pause and finish.
- **App:** the player feeds the tracker; the outbox is flushed on start, when the app becomes
  active and when events are due. Scrobblers a profile hides get nothing from it. Home shows each
  `want-to-read` catalog as "Want to read" right after Continue listening.
- **Add-ons** in `services/catalog`: `/hardcover` (Currently reading with progress, Read, Did not
  finish, rating; Want to read as a catalog with `hardcover:` meta) and `/gpodder` (play actions to
  gpodder.net API 2 or Nextcloud gPodder Sync). Settings are sealed into the URL with
  `CONFIG_SECRET` (AES-256-GCM); nothing is stored. Docs: `/docs/scrobbling/`.
- Device checks: [`m30-follow-ups.md`](../m30-follow-ups.md).
