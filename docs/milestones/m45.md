# M45 Trim silence

- **Chapters from silences** (Android): Player → Chapters → Find chapters on a book without any
  runs a background scan of the book's own files and stores “Part 1”, “Part 2”, … at its long
  silences; Remove takes them away. The rules are in `packages/core/src/library/silence.ts`, the
  scan in `modules/library` (`SilenceScanner.kt`, `SilenceJob.kt`), resumable from a checkpoint and
  paused when the battery is low. The web does not offer it (see Chapters in
  [Decisions](../brief.md#decisions)).
- **Intro and outro:** seconds to skip per show and profile, synced with the subscription and
  applied by the player itself, so episodes Up Next or the car starts with the app closed skip
  them too (`packages/core/src/podcasts/trim.ts`, migration `0014_trim_silence`).

- [ ] (adb) With the battery under 15 % (`adb shell dumpsys battery set level 10`), a scan waits
      (“Waiting for enough battery.”); lowering it during a scan stops the scan; raising it again
      (for example to 50 %) goes on from where it stopped, not from the start.
- [ ] (adb) A subscribed show with Skip intro 30 s and Skip outro 30 s: a new episode starts at 0:30;
      playing into the last 30 s marks it played and the next in Up Next starts. Paused for more than
      an hour at 0:40 and played again: it starts at 0:30, not earlier.
- [ ] (adb) Seek back into the intro and play: it plays. Seek into the outro: it plays to the end.
- [ ] (adb) Change the intro while an episode of the show is loaded: the outro takes the new value at
      once on Android; the web player takes it the next time the episode is loaded while paused.
