# M115 Series 2

What [M109](m109.md) left out, from its review. Series stay worked out on the device
(`packages/core/src/library/series.ts`); decision: [Series](../brief.md#decisions).

- **Hidden volumes.** A book the profile hid is still read for its series, but only to fill a
  gap: its number is no longer shown as missing, and Next in the series doesn't offer to look for
  it (it goes on to the next book the library lists). Hidden books are not listed, join no two
  series, and a number above the highest listed book makes no gaps. A series of hidden books only
  is not listed.
- **Streamed books on other devices.** The synced book (`BookSnapshot` in
  `packages/core/src/sync/ops.ts`) carries its series and when it was set (`series`, `seriesAt`,
  the first part's time). A new device writes it into the first part's tags; a device that has
  the book takes a newer one. The book's own time is not used, as a download also moves it.
  Older devices send neither field, which changes nothing.
- **A series that goes away.** `addStreamedBook` writes the item's own meta's series every time,
  so a meta that no longer names one clears it. A meta from another item that plays the same
  recording only adds a series.
- **Tag families** (`modules/library`, `IdTags.kt`). The number comes from the same family as the
  name: `SERIES` with `SERIES-PART`, or the movement name with the movement number (`MVNM`/`MVIN`,
  `©mvn`/`©mvi`). `SERIES` with only `MVIN` gives the name without a number; a family with both
  wins over one with only a name.
- **Large libraries.** `listSeries`, `getSeries`, `findSeries`, `seriesOfBook` and `nextInSeries`
  take the library's version; the result is kept per database until the version or the profile
  changes. The Library's Series view, the series page, an item page and Next in the series pass
  it. Timings on the Mac with 10,000 books, a third of them in 400 series
  (`packages/core/bench/series.bench.ts`, next to [M106](m106.md)'s rows): working out every series
  takes about 124 ms, more than the Library's whole first draw (76 ms); for the same version it
  takes under 0.1 ms. The rows have budgets (400 ms and 1 ms) like M106's.
- **Jellyfin.** An audiobook item's `SeriesName` becomes its series
  (`packages/core/src/remote/jellyfin.ts`); Jellyfin gives no number, so such a series has no
  order by number and no gaps. Music items never get one.
- **“In your library?”** Saying yes keeps the work's series with the link, in the new
  `work_links.series` column (migration 0028), which syncs with the link. The book keeps its own
  title and cover. Match with catalog… writes it too; `linkedSeriesByKey` reads the column, then
  the older `meta.series`.
