# M115 Series 2

What [M109](m109.md) left out, from its review.

- **Hidden volumes:** a book the profile hid counts as missing, so its number shows as a gap
  with Look for it, and Next in the series offers to look for it. Count hidden books when working
  out gaps, without listing them.
- **Streamed books on other devices:** a streamed book's series rides on its first part's tags,
  which sync doesn't carry, so another device only knows it once the book is played there. Carry
  the series in the synced book.
- **A series that goes away:** a streamed book added again from a meta that no longer names a
  series keeps the old one.
- **Tag families:** a file with `SERIES` and only a movement number (`MVIN`) pairs the two; take
  the number from the same family as the name.
- **Large libraries:** the Library's Series view, the series page and an item page that names a
  series each work out every series. Keep the result per library version.
- **More sources:** Jellyfin's series for audiobooks, and the series of a book confirmed with
  “In your library?”, which stores no meta today.
