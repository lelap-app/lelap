Lelap is an open-source player for audiobooks and podcasts. It plays, downloads, remembers your place and, if you want, syncs it between your devices with end-to-end encryption. No account, no ads, no tracking.

<b>Built for long listens</b>
<ul>
<li>Plays with the screen off and the app closed, with lock screen, headphone and Bluetooth controls.</li>
<li>During a call it pauses, or keeps going at a lower volume you choose.</li>
<li>Sleep timer that fades out, by minutes or at the end of a chapter or episode; shake for ten more minutes. A bedtime routine can turn the volume down too.</li>
<li>Speed per book, smart rewind after a pause, chapters you can edit, bookmarks with notes, and places you can share as a link.</li>
<li>Voice boost, skip silence with the time it saved, and even loudness, per book or per show.</li>
<li>Finds chapters at the long silences of a book without any, and skips a podcast's intro and outro.</li>
</ul>

<b>Your library</b>
<ul>
<li>Your own files and folders: m4b, mp3, opus, flac, with chapters from the tags and covers from the tags or an image beside the audio.</li>
<li>Your own servers: WebDAV (Nextcloud, ownCloud), S3-compatible storage, Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, Navidrome and other Subsonic servers, Audiobookshelf, and SMB and SFTP shares.</li>
<li>Public-domain audiobooks from LibriVox, and podcasts by RSS with OPML import and export.</li>
<li>The library opens in three groups: in progress, not started and finished.</li>
<li>Tags, hand-made lists and smart lists; Up Next for books and episodes.</li>
<li>Hide what you don't want in the library; files that look like music are pointed out.</li>
<li>Stats: time listened, streaks, finished books, and your year in sound to share as an image.</li>
<li>Downloads over Wi-Fi with the screen off, to an SD card if you like; airplane mode is a normal day.</li>
</ul>

<b>Around the house</b>
<ul>
<li>Casts to Sonos, DLNA and Google Cast speakers through open protocols, and to FCast receivers. The lock screen, headphones and sleep timer control the speaker.</li>
<li>A car mode with a large cover and big buttons, Android Auto with your covers, a home screen widget and a Quick Settings tile.</li>
<li>Profiles for everyone in the household, with an optional PIN and a kids profile.</li>
<li>Works with TalkBack and at the largest text size, and uses the width of a tablet.</li>
<li>In English and Bahasa Melayu.</li>
</ul>

<b>Open by design</b>
Catalogs, metadata and audio sources come from add-ons: small web services anyone can run. Lelap ships with add-ons for open sources only. Optional add-ons show Hardcover's trending books and your lists, and keep Hardcover and a gPodder server up to date as you listen; set them up in Settings → Add-ons. Audio always goes straight from its source to your phone, never through our servers.

The same app runs in the browser, and the backend can be self-hosted with Docker.
