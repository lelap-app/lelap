Lelap is an open-source player for audiobooks and podcasts. It plays, downloads, remembers your place and, if you want, syncs it between your devices with end-to-end encryption. No account, no ads, no tracking.

<b>Built for long listens</b>
<ul>
<li>Plays with the screen off and the app closed, with lock screen, headphone and Bluetooth controls.</li>
<li>During a call it pauses, or keeps going at a lower volume you choose.</li>
<li>Sleep timer that fades out, by minutes or at the end of a chapter or episode; shake for ten more minutes. A bedtime routine can turn the volume down too, also on a cast speaker.</li>
<li>Speed per book, smart rewind after a pause, chapters you can edit, bookmarks with notes, and places you can share as a link.</li>
<li>A short history of each book's skips and jumps: after a long jump, one tap goes back, also from the notification and Android Auto.</li>
<li>Voice boost, skip silence with the time it saved, and even loudness, per book or per podcast.</li>
<li>Finds chapters at the long silences of a book without any, and skips a podcast's intro and outro.</li>
</ul>

<b>Your library</b>
<ul>
<li>Your own files and folders: m4b, mp3, opus, flac, with chapters from the tags and covers from the tags or an image beside the audio.</li>
<li>Your own servers: WebDAV (Nextcloud, ownCloud), S3-compatible storage, Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, Navidrome and other Subsonic servers, Audiobookshelf, OPDS catalogs such as Calibre's content server, and SMB and SFTP shares, each with a home and an away address if you like.</li>
<li>Public-domain audiobooks from LibriVox, and podcasts by RSS with OPML import and export. When a feed offers several files of an episode, the smallest plays on mobile data.</li>
<li>The library opens in three groups: in progress, not started and finished.</li>
<li>Tags, hand-made lists and smart lists that play from start to end; Up next for books and episodes.</li>
<li>Whole series in reading order where Wikidata knows them, with the books you don't have yet by their titles, and the next book when one ends.</li>
<li>Rate books in half stars; new podcast episodes are marked.</li>
<li>Hide what you don't want in the library; files that look like music are pointed out.</li>
<li>Stats: time listened, streaks, finished books, and your year in sound to share as an image.</li>
<li>Downloads over Wi-Fi with the screen off, to an SD card if you like; airplane mode is a normal day.</li>
</ul>

<b>Around the house</b>
<ul>
<li>Casts to Sonos, DLNA and Google Cast speakers through open protocols, and to FCast receivers, one speaker or several at once, with your sound settings, and remembers the speakers you played on together. The lock screen, headphones and sleep timer control them.</li>
<li>A car mode with a large cover and big buttons, upright or sideways, Android Auto with your covers and lists and search by voice, a home screen widget and a Quick Settings tile.</li>
<li>Profiles for everyone in the household, with an optional PIN and a kids profile.</li>
<li>Five themes, including Sepia and High contrast, with six accent colours, or follow the phone's light or dark setting.</li>
<li>Works with TalkBack and at the largest text size, and uses the width of a tablet or a foldable.</li>
<li>In English and Bahasa Melayu.</li>
</ul>

<b>Open by design</b>
Catalogs, metadata and audio sources come from add-ons: small web services anyone can run. Lelap ships with add-ons for open sources only. Optional add-ons bring complete Open Yale Courses lectures with transcripts, show your public BookWyrm shelves, show Hardcover's trending books and your lists, and keep Hardcover (shelves and ratings) and a gPodder server up to date as you listen; set them up in Settings → Library and sources → Add-ons. Audio always goes straight from its source to your phone, never through our servers.

The same app runs in the browser, and the backend can be self-hosted with Docker.
